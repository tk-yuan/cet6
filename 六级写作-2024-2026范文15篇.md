## 2024年6月 第1套 · 自主学习

Nowadays, cultivating independent learning ability is becoming increasingly crucial for personal development. In today's fast-changing society, independent learning plays a vital role not only in passing exams but also in surviving every change that follows. From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond the classroom.

To begin with, independent learning can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success. A good case in point is a student who reads one article on a new topic every day; within a year he can explain things his teachers never mentioned, while a classmate who waits to be taught gets nothing.

Moreover, independent learning lays a solid foundation for long-term development, and the earlier it is built, the more it pays. Without it, even a talented student would find his plans turning into excuses within a month.

Meanwhile, from a pragmatic standpoint, independent learning is a cost effective and sustainable option. Talent decides where you start; habit decides where you stop.

In conclusion, independent learning contributes not only to academic success but also to a lasting mind. Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping our overall development.

**中文**
如今，培养自主学习能力对个人发展越来越关键。在当今快速变化的社会里，自主学习不仅在考试中重要，在之后每一次变化中也重要。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出课堂。

首先，自主学习能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。一个典型例子是：一个每天读一篇新领域文章的学生，一年内就能讲出老师从没提过的东西；而等着被教的同学什么也得不到。

其次，自主学习为长远发展打下扎实基础，而且建得越早，回报越多。没有它，即使有天赋的学生也会在一个月内把计划变成借口。

同时，从务实的角度看，自主学习是一个划算且可持续的选择。天赋决定你从哪里起步，习惯决定你在哪里停下。

总之，自主学习不仅有助于学业成功，也有助于一颗持久的心智。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们的整体发展中举足轻重。
