# 六级写作 · 四类 × 六维（讲义原句 + 四篇成品）

## 一、这套怎么运转

讲义的核心不是模板，是**六个论证维度**。你写不出东西，是因为手里没有"某个维度该怎么说"的句子。

```
四类题目 → 每类固定配三个维度 → 每个维度背三句 → 考场把三组句子按顺序摆好
```

| 类别 | 常用三维 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 个人成长 | ①个人成长 + ⑥知识思维 + ④经济现实 | 讲能力、讲思维、讲前途 |
| 环境保护 | ⑤环境可持续 + ①个人成长 + ④经济现实 | 讲未来、讲健康、讲成本 |
| 科技数字化 | ③科技效率 + ⑥知识思维 + ②人际社会 | 讲效率、讲判断、讲关系 |
| 社会 | ②人际社会 + ④经济现实 + ①个人成长 | 讲关系、讲成本、讲个人 |

---

## 二、六维核心句库（第 1 句即讲义原句）

**每维三句：★1 是讲义原句，写作用它；2、3 是补充。**

### ① 个人成长维度

★1. **Such an ability can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success.**
　　这样的能力能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。
2. **It lays a solid foundation for our long-term development.**
　　它为我们的长远发展打下扎实基础。
3. **A person who builds it early spends the rest of his life collecting the interest.**
　　早早把它建起来的人，余生都在收取利息。

### ② 人际社会维度

★1. **Such carelessness may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship. In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally.**
　　这种草率会给我们的关系带来负面影响；长远看，还会危害身心健康。
2. **Mutual respect creates a supportive environment that benefits everyone involved.**
　　相互尊重创造出一个支持性的环境，身处其中的人都能受益。
3. **How we treat the person next to us decides whether a group becomes a team or just a crowd.**
　　我们怎么对待身边的人，决定一个群体是变成团队，还是只是一群人。

### ③ 科技效率维度

★1. **We are at a point where mastering these tools is less of an advantage and more of a basic requirement for full participation in society.**
　　我们已经到了这个节点：掌握这些工具不再算优势，而是参与社会的基本要求。
2. **The same tool that saves us an hour can quietly take away our judgement.**
　　同一件工具能为我们省下一小时，也可能悄悄拿走我们的判断力。
3. **Efficiency without direction only helps us do the wrong thing faster.**
　　没有方向的效率，只会让我们更快地把事情做错。

### ④ 经济现实维度

★1. **From a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option.**
　　从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。
2. **In the long run, the price of ignoring it is far higher than the cost of preparing for it.**
　　从长远看，忽视它的代价远高于为它做准备的代价。
3. **A decision that looks cheap today often sends its bill to someone else tomorrow.**
　　今天看起来便宜的决定，往往把账单寄给了明天的别人。

### ⑤ 环境可持续维度

★1. **Adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle is an imperative for safeguarding our future generations.**
　　采取环保的生活方式，是保护子孙后代的当务之急。
2. **Resources are finite, and a habit built today decides what is left tomorrow.**
　　资源有限，今天养成的习惯决定了明天还剩多少。
3. **The earth does not send us an invoice; it simply keeps a record.**
　　地球不会给我们寄账单，它只是默默记账。

### ⑥ 知识思维维度

★1. **In the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views.**
　　在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息与极端观点的第一道防线。
2. **Reading widely is what separates a person with opinions from a person with judgement.**
　　广泛阅读，把"有观点的人"和"有判断力的人"区分开来。
3. **A mind that never asks a second question is easy to lead anywhere.**
　　从不追问第二个问题的头脑，很容易被带向任何地方。

## 三、四篇成品（论点句全部取自讲义）

### A · 个人成长类（① ⑥ ④）

Nowadays, cultivating independent learning ability is becoming increasingly crucial for personal development. In today's fast-changing society, the ability to teach oneself plays a vital role not only in passing exams but also in surviving every change that follows. From where I stand, it deserves far more attention than it usually receives.

To begin with, such an ability can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success. A student who can read a manual and follow a tutorial picks up a new tool in a week, while a classmate who waits to be taught sits idle for a month.

Moreover, in the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views. Independent learning trains exactly that habit: a reader who checks a source before believing it is far harder to mislead.

Finally, from a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option. A habit built in college keeps paying for decades, while a skill bought at the last minute expires almost at once.

In conclusion, independent learning contributes not only to personal success but also to a lasting mind. Its value, whether visible or quiet, carries significant weight in shaping our overall development.

**中文**
如今，培养自主学习能力对个人发展越来越关键。在当今快速变化的社会里，自学能力不仅在考试中重要，在之后每一次变化中也重要。在我看来，它值得比现在多得多的重视。

首先，这样的能力能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。一个会读手册、会跟教程的学生，一周就能上手新工具；而等着被教的同学，一个月都在空等。

其次，在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息和极端观点的第一道防线。自主学习训练的正是这一习惯：转发前先核实来源的读者，很难被骗。

最后，从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。大学里养成的习惯能回报几十年，而临时抱佛脚学来的技能，几乎立刻就过期。

总之，自主学习不仅有助于个人成功，也塑造一颗持久的心智。它的价值无论显见还是无声，都在塑造我们的整体发展中举足轻重。

---

### B · 环境保护类（⑤ ① ④）

With the trend of urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for nature. In an era of concrete and screens, the young are losing contact with the world outside. From where I stand, this is a loss we cannot afford.

To begin with, adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle is an imperative for safeguarding our future generations. A student who walks to school instead of taking a car saves more energy in a year than any single campaign can.

Moreover, contact with nature can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success. A young person who has watched a river change across four seasons learns patience that no classroom can teach.

Finally, from a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option. Cities that keep green space spend less on health care and attract more talent than those that do not.

In conclusion, a love for nature contributes not only to a healthier body but also to a saner mind. Its value, whether visible or quiet, carries significant weight in shaping the world we leave behind.

**中文**
随着城镇化推进，城市居民培养对自然的更深热爱至关重要。在一个由水泥和屏幕构成的时代，年轻人正在失去与外部世界的联系。在我看来，这是我们承受不起的损失。

首先，采取环保的生活方式，是保护子孙后代的当务之急。每天走路上学而不是坐车的学生，一年省下的能源超过任何一场宣传活动。

其次，亲近自然能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。一个看着一条河走过四季的年轻人，学到的是课堂教不出来的耐心。

最后，从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。保留绿地的城市在医疗上花费更少，吸引的人才更多。

总之，热爱自然不仅有助于健康的身体，也有助于清醒的心智。它的价值无论显见还是无声，都在塑造我们留给后代的世界中举足轻重。

---

### C · 科技数字化类（③ ⑥ ②）

There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and skills in today's world. In an age when a phone can answer almost any question, knowing how to use it well has become a form of basic literacy. From where I stand, this deserves far more attention than it usually receives.

To begin with, we are at a point where mastering these tools is less of an advantage and more of a basic requirement for full participation in society. A clerk who can clean a spreadsheet and write a formula finishes in an hour what a colleague needs a week to do by hand.

Moreover, in the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views. The same tool that saves us an hour can quietly take away our judgement.

Finally, careless use may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship. In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally. A single careless comment online can damage a friendship that took years to build.

In conclusion, digital skills contribute not only to efficiency but also to sound judgement. Their influence, whether visible or quiet, carries significant weight in shaping how we live and work.

**中文**
当今世界，人们越来越意识到数字素养与技能的重要性。在一个手机几乎能回答任何问题的时代，懂得如何用好它，已经成了一种基本素养。在我看来，它值得比现在多得多的重视。

首先，我们已经到了这个节点：掌握这些工具不再算优势，而是参与社会的基本要求。会清洗表格、会写公式的职员，一小时就做完同事手工要一周的活。

其次，在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息和极端观点的第一道防线。同一件工具能为我们省下一小时，也可能悄悄拿走我们的判断力。

最后，草率使用会给我们的关系带来负面影响；长远看，还会危害身心健康。网络上一条轻率的评论，就能毁掉一段花了几年建立起来的友谊。

总之，数字技能不仅有助于效率，也有助于清醒的判断。它的影响无论显见还是无声，都在塑造我们如何生活与工作中举足轻重。

### D · 社会类（② ④ ①）

In an era of rapid social development, it is vitally important to enhance young people's sense of responsibility. In a society where everyone looks after his own affairs, the willingness to take on one more task has become rare. From where I stand, it is exactly what we need most.

To begin with, the absence of such a habit may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship. In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally. A class where nobody is willing to help out soon becomes a group of strangers sitting in the same room.

Moreover, from a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option. Every hour a volunteer gives saves the public purse far more, and the young people involved gain experience no lecture can offer.

Finally, taking responsibility can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success. A student trusted with a real task hesitates less when the community needs a hand.

In conclusion, a sense of responsibility contributes not only to a better society but also to a stronger self. Its value, whether visible or quiet, carries significant weight in shaping the world we share.

**中文**
在社会快速发展的时代，增强青年的责任感至关重要。在一个各人自扫门前雪的社会里，愿意多担一件事反而变得稀缺。在我看来，这恰恰是我们最需要的。

首先，缺少这样的习惯会给我们的关系带来负面影响；长远看，还会危害身心健康。一个没人愿意搭把手的班级，很快就会变成一群坐在同一间屋子里的陌生人。

其次，从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。志愿者付出的每一小时，为公共财政省下的远不止这一小时，参与的年轻人也获得了课堂给不了的经验。

最后，承担责任能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。被托付过真任务的学生，在社区需要帮忙时更少犹豫。

总之，责任感不仅有助于更好的社会，也有助于更强的自己。它的价值无论显见还是无声，都在塑造我们共同的世界中举足轻重。

## 四、考场怎么用（三步）

```
第 1 步  判断类别（个人成长 / 环境 / 科技 / 社会）——30 秒
第 2 步  调出该类三个维度，按 To begin with / Moreover / Finally 摆好
第 3 步  每点先写讲义原句，再补一句自己的例子
```

**例子写不出来时的保命句式**：

> `A student who …, while a classmate who ….`
> 前半句"做了的人"，后半句"没做的人"，任何维度都能套。

**注意**：讲义原句里有 `conducive to`、`imperative`、`polarized` 这类词，拼不准就换普通词（`leads to` / `necessary`），但**不要改句子的骨架**。

## 五、换题示例

| 题目 | 类别 | 三个维度 | 只换什么 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 自律 | 个人成长 | ①⑥④ | ability → self-discipline |
| 心理健康 | 个人成长 | ①⑥④ | ability → a calm mind |
| 网络假新闻 | 科技 | ③⑥② | tools → news，加 verify |
| 追星 | 社会 | ②④① | 把 respect 换成 the right model |
| 脱贫 | 社会 | ②④① | 加 village / road / income |
| 城镇化 | 环境 | ⑤①④ | 加 green space / city |

**讲义原句一个字不改，只改中间那个名词。**
