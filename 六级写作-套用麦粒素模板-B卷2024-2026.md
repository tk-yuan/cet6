## 2024年6月 第1套 · 自主学习（正面）

Nowadays, cultivating independent learning ability is becoming increasingly crucial for personal development. Could you imagine how utterly distinct a graduate's first job would be without the ability to teach himself? Admittedly, independent learning is a complicated and lengthy process, but the achievements often bring tremendous social and economic benefits.

The pluses associated with this topic are numerous and substantial, yielding profound implications if widely adopted and sustainable over the long run. First, in terms of personal abilities, the workplace hands out no syllabus, so a newcomer must read the manual himself. A good case in point is a graduate who learns an unfamiliar software in a week by watching tutorials, while a classmate who waits for a training session is the last to be trusted with real work. Second, in contrast to knowledge memorised for an exam, a method of learning keeps producing new skills. Third, without such a method, education really does end on graduation day.

It is highly necessary for students to take one online course each term without a teacher's supervision. It would do better if universities rewarded the habit before the job market demands it.

**译文**

如今，培养自主学习能力对个人发展越来越关键。如果没有自学能力，一个毕业生第一份工作会多么不同？诚然，自主学习是一个复杂而漫长的过程，但它带来的成就往往会产生巨大的社会和经济效益。

与本主题相关的好处众多且重大，若被广泛采用并长期持续，将产生深远影响。就个人能力而言，职场不发教学大纲，新人必须自己读手册。一个典型例子是：毕业生靠看教程一周就学会不熟悉的软件，而等着被培训的同学，是最后一个被托付真活儿的。与为考试记住的知识相比，学习方法会不断产出新技能。没有这样的方法，教育就真的在毕业那天结束了。

学生极有必要每学期自己选一门没有老师盯的网课。如果大学在就业市场提出要求之前就奖励这个习惯，情况会更好。

用户极有必要给自己定一条规矩：没有来源不转发。如果平台放慢未经核实内容的传播速度，情况会更好。

**译文**
