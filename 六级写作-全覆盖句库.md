# 六级写作 · 全覆盖句库（编号版）

## 一、编号规则

作文里每一句都有编号，考场上照着编号往下写就行，不需要现造句子。

| 编号 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| **K1–K3** | 开头三句 |
| **E1–E2** | 结尾两句 |
| **①–⑥** | 六个论证维度 |
| **A / B** | 该维度的两个分论点 |
| **D** | 展开说明（Development） |
| **R** | 原因（Reason） |
| **Eg** | 举例模板（Example，填人名/身份即可） |
| **M** | 措施（Measure） |
| **T** | 连接词 |

**一篇文章的骨架**：K1 + K2 + K3 + 三个〔T + 维度句〕+ E1 + E2。

---

## 二、开头（K1–K3）

**K1**（抄题目给的句子，一字不改，不用背）

**K2**
> In today's fast-changing society, **X** plays a vital role not only in ______ but also in ______.
> 在当今快速变化的社会里，X 不仅在______上重要，在______上也很重要。

**K3**
> From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond ______.
> 在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出______。

> K2 两个空格填"一个具体场景 + 一个更高的层面"（如 academic success / the kind of adult we become）。
> K3 的空格填"一个具体范围"（如 the classroom / a weekend trip / operating a device）。

---

## 三、结尾（E1–E2）

**E1**
> In conclusion, **X** contributes not only to ______ but also to ______.
> 总之，X 不仅有助于______，也有助于______。

**E2**
> Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping our overall development.
> 它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们的整体发展中举足轻重。

> E2 最后三个词可按题替换：our overall development / the world we leave behind / how we live and work / the world we share。

## 四、六维句库（每维六码）

### ① 个人成长维度

**①A**（分论点一，讲义原句）
> Such an ability can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success.
> 这样的能力能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。

**①B**（分论点二）
> It lays a solid foundation for our long-term development, and the earlier it is built, the more it pays.
> 它为长远发展打下扎实基础；建得越早，回报越多。

**①D**（说明）
> The same effort spent early saves far more time later, because skills build on each other instead of starting from zero.
> 同样一份力气，早花出去能省下后来更多时间，因为技能是一层层叠上去的，不是每次都从零开始。

**①R**（原因，讲义句式）
> The main contributing factor toward this gap is that people want results before they are willing to practise.
> 造成这一差距的主要因素是：人们想在肯练之前先看到结果。

**①Eg**（举例模板）
> A good case in point is a student who ______ every day; within a year he can ______, while a classmate who waits for ______ gets nothing.
> 一个典型例子是：每天______的学生，一年内就能______；而等着______的同学什么也得不到。

**①M**（措施）
> It is highly necessary for schools to ______, because a habit formed early costs far less than a skill bought late.
> 学校极有必要______，因为早养成的习惯，比晚买的技能便宜得多。

### ② 人际社会维度

**②A**（讲义原句）
> Such carelessness may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship.
> 这种草率会给我们的关系带来负面影响。

**②B**（讲义原句）
> In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally.
> 从长远看，它还可能对我们的身心健康构成挑战。

**②D**（说明）
> A relationship takes years to build and one sentence to damage, so the cost of carelessness is always higher than it looks.
> 一段关系要几年才能建立，却只要一句话就能毁掉，所以草率的代价总比看上去更高。

**②R**（原因）
> It is related to the fact that people cannot see the face of the person they are hurting.
> 这与一个事实有关：人们看不见被自己伤害那个人的脸。

**②Eg**（举例模板）
> A good case in point is a student who ______ and loses the trust of ______, while one who ______ keeps it for years.
> 一个典型例子是：一个______的学生失去了______的信任；而一个______的人，把这份信任守了很多年。

**②M**（措施）
> It is highly necessary for schools to teach ______ as a basic skill, because rules alone cannot replace a habit.
> 学校极有必要把______当作基本技能来教，因为光有规矩替代不了习惯。

### ③ 科技效率维度

**③A**（讲义原句）
> We are at a point where mastering these tools is less of an advantage and more of a basic requirement for full participation in society.
> 我们已经到了这个节点：掌握这些工具不再算优势，而是充分参与社会的基本要求。

**③B**（分论点二）
> The same tool that saves us an hour can quietly take away our judgement.
> 同一件工具能为我们省下一小时，也可能悄悄拿走我们的判断力。

**③D**（说明）
> Convenience is useful only when we still know what we are doing with it.
> 只有在我们还清楚自己拿它做什么的时候，便利才算有用。

**③R**（原因）
> The main contributing factor toward this tendency is that tools are designed to be easy, and easy things are rarely done with care.
> 造成这一倾向的主要因素是：工具生来就是为了省事，而省事的事情很少被认真对待。

**③Eg**（举例模板）
> A good case in point is a worker who ______ and finishes in an hour what a colleague needs a week to do by hand.
> 一个典型例子是：会______的工人，一小时就做完同事手工要一周的活。

**③M**（措施）
> It is highly necessary for schools to teach students to use tools rather than merely to operate them, because operation is a skill of the hand and use is a skill of the mind.
> 学校极有必要教学生去"用"工具，而不只是会"操作"，因为操作是手上的技能，使用是头脑的技能。

### ④ 经济现实维度

**④A**（讲义原句）
> From a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option.
> 从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。

**④B**（分论点二）
> In the long run, the price of ignoring it is far higher than the cost of preparing for it.
> 从长远看，忽视它的代价远高于为它做准备的代价。

**④D**（说明）
> What looks cheap today often sends its bill to someone else tomorrow.
> 今天看起来便宜的东西，往往把账单寄给了明天的别人。

**④R**（原因）
> It is related to the fact that the bill never arrives on the same day as the decision, so the cost is easy to ignore.
> 这与一个事实有关：账单从来不在做决定的那天寄到，所以成本很容易被忽视。

**④Eg**（举例模板）
> A good case in point is a city that spends on ______ and saves far more on ______ within ten years.
> 一个典型例子是：在______上投钱的城市，十年内在______上省下的要多得多。

**④M**（措施）
> It is highly necessary for governments to ______, because prevention is always cheaper than repair.
> 政府极有必要______，因为预防永远比修补便宜。

### ⑤ 环境可持续维度

**⑤A**（讲义原句）
> Adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle is an imperative for safeguarding our future generations.
> 采取环保的生活方式，是保护子孙后代的当务之急。

**⑤B**（分论点二）
> Resources are finite, and a habit built today decides what is left tomorrow.
> 资源有限，今天养成的习惯决定了明天还剩多少。

**⑤D**（说明）
> The earth does not send us an invoice; it simply keeps a record.
> 地球不会给我们寄账单，它只是默默记账。

**⑤R**（原因）
> It is related to the fact that environmental costs are spread across millions of people and dozens of years, so no single person feels responsible for them.
> 这与一个事实有关：环境成本分摊在几百万人和几十年里，于是没有一个人觉得自己该负责。

**⑤Eg**（举例模板）
> A good case in point is a student who walks to school instead of taking a car; in a year he saves more energy than a single campaign can.
> 一个典型例子是：走路上学而不是坐车的学生，一年省下的能源超过任何一场宣传活动。

**⑤M**（措施）
> It is highly necessary for cities to keep green space within walking distance, because a habit needs a place to happen.
> 城市极有必要保证步行范围内有绿地，因为习惯需要一个发生的场所。

### ⑥ 知识思维维度

**⑥A**（讲义原句）
> In the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views.
> 在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息与极端观点的第一道防线。

**⑥B**（分论点二）
> Reading widely is what separates a person with opinions from a person with judgement.
> 广泛阅读，把"有观点的人"和"有判断力的人"区分开来。

**⑥D**（说明）
> A mind that never asks a second question is easy to lead anywhere.
> 一个从不追问第二个问题的头脑，很容易被带向任何地方。

**⑥R**（原因）
> The main contributing factor toward this weakness is that algorithms keep showing us what we already agree with.
> 造成这一弱点的主要因素是：算法不断给我们看自己本来就已经赞同的东西。

**⑥Eg**（举例模板）
> A good case in point is a reader who checks the source before sharing and is rarely fooled, while one who trusts the first result is led without noticing.
> 一个典型例子是：转发前核实来源的读者很少被骗；而相信第一条结果的人，往往在不知不觉中被带偏。

**⑥M**（措施）
> It is highly necessary for schools to teach students how to trace a claim to its source, because a habit formed early is hard to break.
> 学校极有必要教学生把一个说法追到源头，因为早年养成的习惯很难改掉。

## 八、四类范文（每句标明编号）

## A · 个人成长类（① ⑥ ④）· 自律

Nowadays, more and more students have realized the importance of self-discipline in their personal growth.〔K1〕 In today's fast-changing society, self-discipline plays a vital role not only in academic success but also in the kind of adult we become.〔K2〕 From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond the classroom.〔K3〕

To begin with, self-discipline can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success.〔T1+①A〕 A good case in point is a student who writes five hundred words every morning; within a year he finishes a short book, while a classmate who waits for inspiration gets nothing.〔①Eg〕 The same effort spent early saves far more time later, because skills build on each other instead of starting from zero.〔①D〕

Moreover, in the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views.〔T2+⑥A〕 Reading widely is what separates a person with opinions from a person with judgement, and self-discipline is what makes the reading happen.〔⑥B〕 The main contributing factor toward this weakness is that algorithms keep showing us what we already agree with.〔⑥R〕

Meanwhile, from a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option.〔T3+④A〕 In the long run, the price of ignoring it is far higher than the cost of preparing for it.〔④B〕

In conclusion, self-discipline contributes not only to academic excellence but also to character formation.〔E1〕 Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping our overall development.〔E2〕

**中文**
如今越来越多的学生意识到自律在个人成长中的重要性。在当今快速变化的社会里，自律不仅在学业上重要，也决定我们会长成什么样的成年人。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出课堂。

首先，自律能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。一个典型例子是：一个每天早上写五百字的学生，一年内能写完一本小书；而等着灵感的同学什么也得不到。同样一份力气，早花出去能省下后来更多时间，因为技能是一层层叠上去的，不是每次都从零开始。

其次，在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息与极端观点的第一道防线。广泛阅读把"有观点的人"和"有判断力的人"区分开来，而自律正是让阅读真正发生的东西。造成这一弱点的主要因素是：算法不断给我们看自己本来就已经赞同的东西。

同时，从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。从长远看，忽视它的代价远高于为它做准备的代价。

总之，自律不仅有助于学业优秀，也有助于品格形成。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们的整体发展中举足轻重。

## B · 环境保护类（⑤ ① ④）· 热爱自然

With the trend of urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for nature.〔K1〕 In today's fast-changing society, a love for nature plays a vital role not only in our physical health but also in the kind of city we build.〔K2〕 From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond a weekend trip.〔K3〕

To begin with, adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle is an imperative for safeguarding our future generations.〔T1+⑤A〕 A good case in point is a student who walks to school instead of taking a car; in a year he saves more energy than a single campaign can.〔⑤Eg〕 The earth does not send us an invoice; it simply keeps a record.〔⑤D〕

Moreover, contact with nature can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success.〔T2+①A〕 A good case in point is a young person who has watched a river change across four seasons; he learns a patience that no classroom can teach.〔①Eg〕

Meanwhile, from a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option.〔T3+④A〕 It is related to the fact that environmental costs are spread across millions of people and dozens of years, so no single person feels responsible for them.〔④R〕

In conclusion, a love for nature contributes not only to a healthier body but also to a saner mind.〔E1〕 Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping the world we leave behind.〔E2〕

**中文**
随着城镇化推进，城市居民培养对自然的更深热爱至关重要。在当今快速变化的社会里，热爱自然不仅关乎身体健康，也关乎我们会建成什么样的城市。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出一个周末的郊游。

首先，采取环保的生活方式，是保护子孙后代的当务之急。一个典型例子是：走路上学而不是坐车的学生，一年省下的能源超过任何一场宣传活动。地球不会给我们寄账单，它只是默默记账。

其次，亲近自然能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。一个典型例子是：一个看着一条河走过四季的年轻人，学到的是课堂教不出来的耐心。

同时，从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。这与一个事实有关：环境成本分摊在几百万人和几十年里，于是没有一个人觉得自己该负责。

总之，热爱自然不仅有助于更健康的身体，也有助于更清醒的心智。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们留给后代的世界中举足轻重。

## C · 科技数字化类（③ ⑥ ②）· 数字素养

There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and skills in today's world.〔K1〕 In today's fast-changing society, digital literacy plays a vital role not only in finding a job but also in telling truth from noise.〔K2〕 From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond operating a device.〔K3〕

To begin with, we are at a point where mastering these tools is less of an advantage and more of a basic requirement for full participation in society.〔T1+③A〕 A good case in point is a worker who can clean a spreadsheet and write a formula; he finishes in an hour what a colleague needs a week to do by hand.〔③Eg〕 Convenience is useful only when we still know what we are doing with it.〔③D〕

Moreover, in the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views.〔T2+⑥A〕 A good case in point is a reader who checks the source before sharing and is rarely fooled, while one who trusts the first result is led without noticing.〔⑥Eg〕

Meanwhile, careless use may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship.〔T3+②A〕 In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally.〔②B〕

In conclusion, digital literacy contributes not only to efficiency but also to sound judgement.〔E1〕 Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping how we live and work.〔E2〕

**中文**
当今世界，人们越来越意识到数字素养与技能的重要性。在当今快速变化的社会里，数字素养不仅在求职时重要，也关乎能否分清真相与噪音。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出会操作一台设备。

首先，我们已经到了这个节点：掌握这些工具不再算优势，而是充分参与社会的基本要求。一个典型例子是：一个会清洗表格、会写公式的工人，一小时就做完同事手工要一周的活。只有在我们还清楚自己拿它做什么的时候，便利才算有用。

其次，在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息与极端观点的第一道防线。一个典型例子是：转发前核实来源的读者很少被骗，而相信第一条结果的人往往在不知不觉中被带偏。

同时，草率使用会给我们的关系带来负面影响；从长远看，还可能危害身心健康。

总之，数字素养不仅有助于效率，也有助于清醒的判断。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们如何生活与工作中举足轻重。

## D · 社会类（② ④ ①）· 责任感

In an era of rapid social development, it is vitally important to enhance young people's sense of responsibility.〔K1〕 In today's fast-changing society, a sense of responsibility plays a vital role not only in personal growth but also in the health of a community.〔K2〕 From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond a single task.〔K3〕

Moreover, from a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option.〔T2+④A〕 It is related to the fact that the bill never arrives on the same day as the decision, so the cost is easy to ignore.〔④R〕
