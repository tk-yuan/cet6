# 六级写作 · 全覆盖句库（编号版 · v2）

## 一、这套的结构

```
一个论点 = 1 句【维度分论点】（点明主题）+ 1 句【论证方法】（证明它）
一篇三个论点，三个维度各取一个，三种方法各取一种
```

**规则三条**
1. **举例论证全篇只用一次**，另外两点用反证 / 条件 / 对比 / 原因 / 强调 / 解释。
2. **每个分论点句里必须出现主题词**，不能只写 "such an ability"，否则读者不知道"它"是谁。
3. 一个论点里面的句子**可以来自不同维度**，只要接得上——分论点是骨架，论证方法才是肉。

## 五、六个维度的分论点（每维两句）

> 用法：把句中的 "such an ability / it" 换成**主题词**，写完整。

### ① 个人成长

**①A** Such an ability can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success.
　　这样的能力能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。

**①B** It lays a solid foundation for our long-term development, and the earlier it is built, the more it pays.
　　它为长远发展打下扎实基础；建得越早，回报越多。

**①C** It gives young people a stage on which personal ambition can perform, and the country grows because its people do.
　　它为年轻人提供了施展个人抱负的舞台，而国家之所以成长，是因为它的人在成长。

### ② 人际社会

**②A** Such carelessness may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship.
　　这种草率会给我们的关系带来负面影响。

**②B** In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally.
　　从长远看，它还可能对我们的身心健康构成挑战。

### ③ 科技效率

**③A** We are at a point where mastering these tools is less of an advantage and more of a basic requirement for full participation in society.
　　我们已经到了这个节点：掌握这些工具不再算优势，而是充分参与社会的基本要求。

**③B** The same tool that saves us an hour can quietly take away our judgement.
　　同一件工具能为我们省下一小时，也可能悄悄拿走我们的判断力。

### ④ 经济现实

**④A** From a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option.
　　从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。

**④B** In the long run, the price of ignoring it is far higher than the cost of preparing for it.
　　从长远看，忽视它的代价远高于为它做准备的代价。

**④C** National investment in it returns to ordinary families in the form of better schools and steadier jobs.
　　国家在它上面的投入，会以更好的学校和更稳定的工作回到普通家庭。

### ⑤ 环境可持续

**⑤A** Adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle is an imperative for safeguarding our future generations.
　　采取环保的生活方式，是保护子孙后代的当务之急。

**⑤B** Resources are finite, and a habit built today decides what is left tomorrow.
　　资源有限，今天养成的习惯决定了明天还剩多少。

### ⑥ 知识思维

**⑥A** In the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views.
　　在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息与极端观点的第一道防线。

**⑥B** Reading widely is what separates a person with opinions from a person with judgement.
　　广泛阅读，把"有观点的人"和"有判断力的人"区分开来。

**⑥C** A person who understands where he comes from is far harder to mislead.
　　一个知道自己从哪儿来的人，很难被带偏。

## 六、八种论证方法（每法两句成句）

> **一篇只用一次 M1。** 其余两点从 M2–M8 里各挑一种。

### M1 举例（全篇限一次）

**M1a** A good case in point is a student who ______; within a year he can ______, while a classmate who waits for ______ gets nothing.
　　一个典型例子是：一个______的学生，一年内就能______；而等着______的同学什么也得不到。

**M1b** To take an obvious example, a worker who ______ finishes in an hour what a colleague needs a week to do by hand.
　　举个明显的例子：会______的工人，一小时就做完同事手工要一周的活。

### M2 反证

**M2a** Without it, even a talented person would find his plans turning into excuses within a month.
　　没有它，即使有天赋的人也会在一个月内把计划变成借口。

**M2b** Without such a habit, one may work hard for a week and then lose the thread entirely.
　　没有这样的习惯，一个人可能努力一个星期，然后彻底断线。

### M3 条件

**M3a** If a person practises it for one hour a day, he can still finish a long project within a year.
　　如果每天练一小时，一个人一年内仍然能完成一个长项目。

**M3b** Once the habit is formed, the work that once felt heavy becomes almost automatic.
　　一旦习惯形成，过去觉得沉重的工作几乎会变得自动完成。

### M4 原因（讲义句式）

**M4a** The main contributing factor toward this gap is that ______.
　　造成这一差距的主要因素是：______。

**M4b** It is related to the fact that ______.
　　这与一个事实有关：______。

### M5 影响

**M5a** It benefits not only the individual but also the people around him.
　　它不仅让个人受益，也让身边的人受益。

**M5b** Its absence harms both the person himself and those who depend on him.
　　缺少它，既害了他自己，也害了依赖他的人。

### M6 对比

**M6a** Compared with someone who works only when he feels like it, a steady person usually finishes first.
　　与只在想干的时候才干的人相比，稳定的人通常先完成。

**M6b** Talent decides where you start; habit decides where you stop.
　　天赋决定你从哪里起步，习惯决定你在哪里停下。

### M7 强调

**M7a** It is not talent but this daily habit that separates the two.
　　把两者区分开来的，不是天赋，而是这个日常习惯。

**M7b** Indeed, the difference lies less in ability than in repetition.
　　确实，差别与其说在能力，不如说在重复。

### M8 解释

**M8a** In other words, it is less a matter of ability than of choosing to begin.
　　换言之，这与其说是能力问题，不如说是愿不愿意开始的问题。

**M8b** Put simply, the habit is not the reward of success but its cause.
　　简单说，这个习惯不是成功的结果，而是成功的原因。

## 九、四类范文（每句标编号）

## A · 个人成长类 · 自律

Nowadays, more and more students have realized the importance of self-discipline in their personal growth.〔K1〕 In today's fast-changing society, self-discipline plays a vital role not only in academic success but also in the kind of adult we become.〔K2〕 From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond the classroom.〔K3〕

To begin with, self-discipline can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success.〔T1+①A〕 A good case in point is a student who writes five hundred words every morning; within a year he finishes a short book, while a classmate who waits for inspiration gets nothing.〔M1a〕

Moreover, self-discipline lays a solid foundation for long-term development, and the earlier it is built, the more it pays.〔T2+①B〕 Without it, even a talented student would find his plans turning into excuses within a month.〔M2a〕

Meanwhile, from a pragmatic standpoint, self-discipline is a cost effective and sustainable option.〔T3+④A〕 Compared with a clever classmate who works only when he feels like it, a disciplined one usually finishes first.〔M6a〕

In conclusion, self-discipline contributes not only to academic excellence but also to character formation.〔E1〕 Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping our overall development.〔E2〕

**中文**
如今越来越多的学生意识到自律在个人成长中的重要性。在当今快速变化的社会里，自律不仅在学业上重要，也决定我们会长成什么样的成年人。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出课堂。

首先，自律能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。一个典型例子是：一个每天早上写五百字的学生，一年内能写完一本小书；而等着灵感的同学什么也得不到。

其次，自律为长期发展打下扎实基础，而且建得越早，回报越多。没有它，即使有天赋的学生也会在一个月内把计划变成借口。

同时，从务实的角度看，自律是一个划算且可持续的选择。与只在想干的时候才干活的聪明同学相比，自律的人通常先完成。

总之，自律不仅有助于学业优秀，也有助于品格形成。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们的整体发展中举足轻重。

## B · 环境保护类 · 热爱自然

With the trend of urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for nature.〔K1〕 In today's fast-changing society, a love for nature plays a vital role not only in our physical health but also in the kind of city we build.〔K2〕 From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond a weekend trip.〔K3〕

To begin with, adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle is an imperative for safeguarding our future generations.〔T1+⑤A〕 To take an obvious example, a student who walks to school instead of taking a car saves more energy in a year than a single campaign can.〔M1b〕

Moreover, resources are finite, and a habit built today decides what is left tomorrow.〔T2+⑤B〕 Without such a habit, a family may cut waste for a week and then quietly return to its old ways.〔M2b〕

Meanwhile, from a pragmatic standpoint, an environmentally friendly lifestyle is a cost effective and sustainable option.〔T3+④A〕 Compared with cities that build only roads, those that keep green space spend less on health care and attract more talent.〔M6a〕

In conclusion, a love for nature contributes not only to a healthier body but also to a saner mind.〔E1〕 Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping the world we leave behind.〔E2〕

**中文**
随着城镇化推进，城市居民培养对自然的更深热爱至关重要。在当今快速变化的社会里，热爱自然不仅关乎身体健康，也关乎我们会建成什么样的城市。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出一个周末的郊游。

首先，采取环保的生活方式，是保护子孙后代的当务之急。举个明显的例子：走路上学而不是坐车的学生，一年省下的能源超过任何一场宣传活动。

其次，资源是有限的，今天养成的习惯决定了明天还剩多少。没有这样的习惯，一家人可能节约一个星期，然后悄悄地回到老样子。

同时，从务实的角度看，环保的生活方式是一个划算且可持续的选择。与只修路的城市相比，保留绿地的城市在医疗上花费更少，吸引的人才更多。

总之，热爱自然不仅有助于更健康的身体，也有助于更清醒的心智。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们留给后代的世界中举足轻重。

## C · 科技数字化类 · 数字素养

There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and skills in today's world.〔K1〕 In today's fast-changing society, digital literacy plays a vital role not only in finding a job but also in telling truth from noise.〔K2〕 From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond operating a device.〔K3〕

To begin with, we are at a point where mastering these tools is less of an advantage and more of a basic requirement for full participation in society.〔T1+③A〕 To take an obvious example, a worker who can clean a spreadsheet and write a formula finishes in an hour what a colleague needs a week to do by hand.〔M1b〕

Moreover, the same tool that saves us an hour can quietly take away our judgement.〔T2+③B〕 Without it, a reader may accept the first search result and never learn that he was misled.〔M2a〕

Meanwhile, careless use may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship.〔T3+②A〕 In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally.〔②B〕

In conclusion, digital literacy contributes not only to efficiency but also to sound judgement.〔E1〕 Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping how we live and work.〔E2〕

**中文**
当今世界，人们越来越意识到数字素养与技能的重要性。在当今快速变化的社会里，数字素养不仅在求职时重要，也关乎能否分清真相与噪音。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出会操作一台设备。

首先，我们已经到了这个节点：掌握这些工具不再算优势，而是充分参与社会的基本要求。举个明显的例子：会清洗表格、会写公式的工人，一小时就做完同事手工要一周的活。

其次，同一件工具能为我们省下一小时，也可能悄悄拿走我们的判断力。没有它，读者可能接受第一条搜索结果，而且永远不知道自己被误导过。

同时，草率使用会给我们的关系带来负面影响。从长远看，它还可能危害身心健康。

总之，数字素养不仅有助于效率，也有助于清醒的判断。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们如何生活与工作中举足轻重。

## D · 社会类 · 责任感

In an era of rapid social development, it is vitally important to enhance young people's sense of responsibility.〔K1〕 In today's fast-changing society, a sense of responsibility plays a vital role not only in personal growth but also in the health of a community.〔K2〕 From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond a single task.〔K3〕

To begin with, a sense of responsibility can sharpen young people's competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for their overall development.〔T1+①A〕 A good case in point is a student who runs a weekend class for children in her neighbourhood; within a year she is the one everyone trusts with a real task.〔M1a〕

Moreover, indifference may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship.〔T2+②A〕 Without such a habit, a young person learns that nothing outside his own scores is his business, and hesitates the first time the community needs a hand.〔M2b〕

Meanwhile, from a pragmatic standpoint, a sense of responsibility is a cost effective and sustainable option.〔T3+④A〕 It is related to the fact that the reward never arrives on the same day as the effort, which is why so many people give up early.〔M4b〕

In conclusion, a sense of responsibility contributes not only to a better society but also to a stronger self.〔E1〕 Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping the world we share.〔E2〕

**中文**
在社会快速发展的时代，增强青年的责任感至关重要。在当今快速变化的社会里，责任感不仅关乎个人成长，也关乎一个社区的健康。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出完成一件任务本身。

首先，责任感能增强年轻人的竞争力，有助于为他们的全面发展铺路。一个典型例子是：给社区孩子办周末班的学生，一年后成了大家最愿意托付真任务的人。

其次，冷漠会给我们的关系带来负面影响。没有这样的习惯，年轻人会学到"分数之外的事与我无关"，第一次社区需要帮忙时就犹豫了。

同时，从务实的角度看，责任感是一个划算且可持续的选择。这与一个事实有关：回报从来不在付出的同一天到来，这正是很多人早早放弃的原因。

总之，责任感不仅有助于更好的社会，也有助于更强的自己。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们共同的世界中举足轻重。
