# 六维语料库（每维五类：分论点 / 说明 / 原因 / 举例 / 措施）

## 怎么用

每一维都是一个"抽屉"，里面按功能分好格子。写第二段时：

```
分论点 → 举例 → 说明        （一个论点写完整，约 45 词）
```

题目要你提建议时，再加：

```
措施 → 原因                （约 30 词，放在第二段最后一点）
```

**分论点只选两个不同维度**，不要两个都从同一维度拿。

---

## ① 个人成长维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> Such an ability can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success.
> 这样的能力能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。

**【分论点 B】**
> It lays a solid foundation for our long-term development, and the earlier it is built, the more it pays.
> 它为我们的长远发展打下扎实基础；而且建得越早，回报越多。

**【展开说明】**
> The same effort spent early saves far more time later, because skills build on each other instead of starting from zero.
> 同样一份力气，早花出去能省下后来更多时间，因为技能是一层层叠上去的，不是每次都从零开始。

**【原因】**
> Most people fail here not because they lack ability, but because they want results before they are willing to practise.
> 大多数人败在这里，不是因为没有能力，而是想在肯练之前先看到结果。

**【举例模板】**
> A student who ______ every day can ______ in a year, while a classmate who waits for ______ gets nothing.
> 一个每天______的学生，一年后能______；而等着______的同学什么也得不到。

**【措施】**
> It is highly necessary for schools to ______, because a habit formed early costs far less than a skill bought late.
> 学校极有必要______，因为早养成的习惯，比晚花钱买来的技能便宜得多。

## ② 人际社会维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> Such carelessness may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship.
> 这种草率会给我们的关系带来负面影响。

**【分论点 B】★讲义原句**
> In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally.
> 从长远看，它还可能对我们的身心健康构成挑战。

**【展开说明】**
> A relationship takes years to build and one sentence to damage, so the cost of carelessness is always higher than it looks.
> 一段关系要几年才能建立，却只要一句话就能毁掉，所以草率的代价总比看上去更高。

**【原因】**
> People act carelessly mainly because they cannot see the face of the person they are hurting.
> 人们之所以草率，主要是因为他们看不见被伤害那个人的脸。

**【举例模板】**
> A student who ______ loses the trust of ______, while one who ______ keeps it for years.
> 一个______的学生会失去______的信任；而一个______的人，能把这份信任守住很多年。

**【措施】**
> It is highly necessary for schools to teach ______ as a basic skill, because rules alone cannot replace a habit.
> 学校极有必要把______当作一项基本技能来教，因为光有规矩替代不了习惯。

## ③ 科技效率维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> We are at a point where mastering these tools is less of an advantage and more of a basic requirement for full participation in society.
> 我们已经到了这个节点：掌握这些工具不再算优势，而是充分参与社会的基本要求。

**【分论点 B】**
> The same tool that saves us an hour can quietly take away our judgement.
> 同一件工具能为我们省下一小时，也可能悄悄拿走我们的判断力。

**【展开说明】**
> Convenience is useful only when we still know what we are doing with it.
> 只有在我们还清楚自己拿它做什么的时候，便利才算有用。

**【原因】**
> Tools are designed to be easy, and easy things are rarely done with care.
> 工具生来就是为了省事，而省事的事情很少被认真对待。

**【举例模板】**
> A worker who ______ finishes in an hour what a colleague needs a week to do by hand.
> 会______的职员，一小时就能做完同事手工要一周的活。

**【措施】**
> It is highly necessary for schools to teach students to use tools rather than merely to operate them, because operation is a skill of the hand and use is a skill of the mind.
> 学校极有必要教学生去"用"工具，而不只是会"操作"工具，因为操作是手上的技能，使用是头脑的技能。

## ④ 经济现实维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> From a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option.
> 从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。

**【分论点 B】**
> In the long run, the price of ignoring it is far higher than the cost of preparing for it.
> 从长远看，忽视它的代价远高于为它做准备的代价。

**【展开说明】**
> What looks cheap today often sends its bill to someone else tomorrow.
> 今天看起来便宜的东西，往往把账单寄给了明天的别人。

**【原因】**
> People underestimate long-term cost because the bill never arrives on the same day as the decision.
> 人们之所以低估长期成本，是因为账单从来不在做决定的那天寄到。

**【举例模板】**
> A city that spends on ______ saves far more on ______ within ten years.
> 一个在______上花钱的城市，十年内在______上省下的要多得多。

**【措施】**
> It is highly necessary for governments to ______, because prevention is always cheaper than repair.
> 政府极有必要______，因为预防永远比修补便宜。

## ⑤ 环境可持续维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> Adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle is an imperative for safeguarding our future generations.
> 采取环保的生活方式，是保护子孙后代的当务之急。

**【分论点 B】**
> Resources are finite, and a habit built today decides what is left tomorrow.
> 资源是有限的，今天养成的习惯决定了明天还剩多少。

**【展开说明】**
> The earth does not send us an invoice; it simply keeps a record.
> 地球不会给我们寄账单，它只是默默记账。

**【原因】**
> People ignore environmental cost because it is spread across millions of people and dozens of years, so no single person feels responsible for it.
> 人们忽视环境成本，是因为它分摊在几百万人和几十年里，于是没有一个人觉得自己该负责。

**【举例模板】**
> A student who walks to school instead of taking a car saves more energy in a year than a single campaign can.
> 一个走路上学而不是坐车的学生，一年省下的能源超过任何一场宣传活动。

**【措施】**
> It is highly necessary for cities to keep green space within walking distance, because a habit needs a place to happen.
> 城市极有必要保证步行范围内有绿地，因为习惯需要一个发生的场所。

## ⑥ 知识思维维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> In the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views.
> 在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息与极端观点的第一道防线。

**【分论点 B】**
> Reading widely is what separates a person with opinions from a person with judgement.
> 广泛阅读，把"有观点的人"和"有判断力的人"区分开来。

**【展开说明】**
> A mind that never asks a second question is easy to lead anywhere.
> 一个从不追问第二个问题的头脑，很容易被带向任何地方。

**【原因】**
> Algorithms show us what we already agree with, so the habit of doubt has to be trained on purpose.
> 算法只给我们看自己本来就赞同的东西，所以"怀疑"这个习惯必须刻意训练。

**【举例模板】**
> A reader who checks the source before sharing is rarely fooled, while one who trusts the first result is led without noticing.
> 转发前核实来源的读者很少被骗；而相信第一条结果的人，往往在不知不觉中被带偏。

**【措施】**
> It is highly necessary for schools to teach students how to trace a claim to its source, because a habit formed early is hard to break.
> 学校极有必要教学生把一个说法追溯到它的源头，因为早年养成的习惯很难改掉。

## 拼装示范（个人成长类 · 自律）

```
To begin with, self-discipline can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive
to paving the way for our overall development and future success.          ← ①分论点A
A student who writes five hundred words every morning finishes a thesis in a
semester, while a classmate who waits for inspiration gets nothing.         ← ①举例
The same effort spent early saves far more time later.                      ← ①说明

Moreover, reading widely is what separates a person with opinions from a person
with judgement, and self-discipline is what makes the reading happen.        ← ⑥分论点B
A mind that never asks a second question is easy to lead anywhere.          ← ⑥说明

Finally, from a pragmatic standpoint, self-discipline is a cost effective and
sustainable option.                                                          ← ④分论点A
In the long run, the price of ignoring it is far higher than the cost of
preparing for it.                                                            ← ④分论点B
```

**中文**
首先，自律能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。一个每天早上写五百字的学生，一学期就能写完一篇论文；而等着灵感的同学什么也得不到。同样一份力气，早花出去能省下后来更多时间。

其次，广泛阅读把"有观点的人"和"有判断力的人"区分开来，而自律正是让阅读真正发生的东西。一个从不追问第二个问题的头脑，很容易被带向任何地方。

最后，从务实的角度看，自律是一个划算且可持续的选择。从长远看，忽视它的代价远高于为它做准备的代价。

## 五类句子的分工

| 功能 | 放在哪 | 作用 | 中文 | 优先级 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 分论点 | 每点第一句 | 亮观点，最容易拿分 | 每点的核心主张 | ★★★ |
| 举例 | 分论点后 | 证明"我真想过" | 一个具体的人做了什么 | ★★★ |
| 说明 | 例子后 | 把例子和观点连起来 | 为什么会这样 | ★★ |
| 原因 | 想加深度时 | 解释成因，冲高分用 | 背后机制 | ★ |
| 措施 | 题目要建议时 | 放第二段最后一点 | 应该怎么办 | ★★ |

**只有时间背两类的话，背【分论点】和【举例模板】。** 这两类就能撑起一篇 200 词的文章。

# 六、四类范文（开头结尾照讲义句式）

## 统一的头尾（每篇只换括号里的名词）

**开头三句**
```
【抄题目首句】
+ In today's fast-changing society, X plays a vital role not only in A but also in B.
+ From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond ______.
```

**结尾两句**
```
In conclusion, X contributes not only to A but also to B.
Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping
our overall development.
```

**中段三段标记**：To begin with / Moreover / Meanwhile，每段“论点 + Through… + It is not uncommon for…”

---

## A · 个人成长类（① ⑥ ④）· 自律

Nowadays, more and more students have realized the importance of self-discipline in their personal growth. In today's fast-changing society, self-discipline plays a vital role not only in academic success but also in the kind of adult we become. From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond the classroom.

To begin with, self-discipline serves as a cornerstone for students' personal growth and long-term success. Through small decisions repeated every day, it helps young people cultivate resilience, patience and a proactive mindset, all of which lay a solid foundation for future achievement. It is not uncommon for a student who writes five hundred words every morning to finish a thesis in a semester, while a classmate who waits for inspiration writes nothing.

Moreover, self-discipline equips students with the habit of thinking for themselves. In an age of information overload, the ability to judge independently is indispensable, and only a disciplined reader finishes a book instead of a headline. By choosing what to read and when to stop, students broaden their horizons and form their own judgement.

Meanwhile, from a pragmatic standpoint, self-discipline is a cost effective and sustainable option. A habit built in college keeps paying for decades, while a skill bought at the last minute expires almost at once.

In conclusion, self-discipline contributes not only to academic excellence but also to character formation. Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping our overall development.

**中文**
如今越来越多的学生意识到自律在个人成长中的重要性。在当今快速变化的社会里，自律不仅在学业上重要，也决定我们会长成什么样的成年人。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出课堂。

首先，自律是学生个人成长与长期成功的一块基石。通过每天重复的小决定，它帮助年轻人培养韧性、耐心和主动的心态，这一切为未来的成就打下扎实基础。一个每天早上写五百字的学生，一学期就能写完一篇论文，而等着灵感的同学什么也写不出来——这样的事并不少见。

其次，自律让人养成独立思考的习惯。在信息过载的时代，独立判断的能力不可或缺，而只有自律的读者才会读完一本书，而不是只读一个标题。通过选择读什么、什么时候停下，学生拓宽了视野，形成了自己的判断。

同时，从务实的角度看，自律是一个划算且可持续的选择。大学里养成的习惯能回报几十年，而临时抱佛脚学来的技能，几乎立刻就过期。

总之，自律不仅有助于学业优秀，也有助于品格形成。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们的整体发展中举足轻重。

## B · 环境保护类（⑤ ① ④）· 热爱自然

With the trend of urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for nature. In today's fast-changing society, a love for nature plays a vital role not only in our physical health but also in the kind of city we build. From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond a weekend trip.

To begin with, adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle is an imperative for safeguarding our future generations. Through small daily choices, it helps families cut waste and save energy, all of which lay a solid foundation for a livable city. It is not uncommon for a student who walks to school to save more energy in a year than a single campaign can.

Moreover, contact with nature equips young people with patience and curiosity. In an age of screens, the ability to observe something slowly is indispensable. By watching a river change across four seasons, students learn a kind of attention that no classroom can teach.

Meanwhile, from a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option. Cities that keep green space spend less on health care and attract more talent than those that do not.

In conclusion, a love for nature contributes not only to a healthier body but also to a saner mind. Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping the world we leave behind.

**中文**
随着城镇化推进，城市居民培养对自然的更深热爱至关重要。在当今快速变化的社会里，热爱自然不仅关乎身体健康，也关乎我们会建成什么样的城市。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出一个周末的郊游。

首先，采取环保的生活方式，是保护子孙后代的当务之急。通过每天的小选择，它帮助家庭减少浪费、节约能源，这一切为一座宜居的城市打下扎实基础。一个走路上学的学生，一年省下的能源超过任何一场宣传活动——这样的事并不少见。

其次，亲近自然让年轻人拥有耐心和好奇。在一个被屏幕包围的时代，慢慢观察一件事物的能力不可或缺。通过看着一条河走过四季，学生学到一种课堂教不出来的专注。

同时，从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。保留绿地的城市在医疗上花费更少，吸引的人才更多。

总之，热爱自然不仅有助于更健康的身体，也有助于更清醒的心智。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们留给后代的世界中举足轻重。

## C · 科技数字化类（③ ⑥ ②）· 数字素养

There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and skills in today's world. In today's fast-changing society, digital literacy plays a vital role not only in finding a job but also in telling truth from noise. From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond operating a device.

To begin with, we are at a point where mastering these tools is less of an advantage and more of a basic requirement for full participation in society. Through daily use, it helps workers finish in an hour what once took a week, which lays a solid foundation for a productive career. It is not uncommon for a clerk who can write a formula to be trusted with the work that really matters.

Moreover, in the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views. In an age of information overload, the ability to judge independently is indispensable. By checking a source before sharing, students keep a clear head while others are led without noticing.

Meanwhile, careless use may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship. In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally.

In conclusion, digital literacy contributes not only to efficiency but also to sound judgement. Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping how we live and work.

**中文**
当今世界，人们越来越意识到数字素养与技能的重要性。在当今快速变化的社会里，数字素养不仅在求职时重要，也关乎能否分清真相与噪音。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出会操作一台设备。

首先，我们已经到了这个节点：掌握这些工具不再算优势，而是充分参与社会的基本要求。通过日常使用，它让工人一小时做完过去要一周的活，这为一份高效的职业生涯打下扎实基础。会写公式的职员被托付真正重要的活儿——这样的事并不少见。

其次，在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息与极端观点的第一道防线。在信息过载的时代，独立判断的能力不可或缺。转发前核实来源的学生保持清醒，而别人在不知不觉中被带偏。

同时，草率使用会给我们的关系带来负面影响；从长远看，还可能危害身心健康。

总之，数字素养不仅有助于效率，也有助于清醒的判断。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们如何生活与工作中举足轻重。

## D · 社会类（② ④ ①）· 责任感

In an era of rapid social development, it is vitally important to enhance young people's sense of responsibility. In today's fast-changing society, a sense of responsibility plays a vital role not only in personal growth but also in the health of a community. From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond a single task.

To begin with, a sense of responsibility serves as a cornerstone for a healthy community. Through small acts of help, it builds a trust that no rule can create. It is not uncommon for a class where nobody is willing to help out to become a group of strangers sitting in the same room.

Moreover, from a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option. Every hour a volunteer gives saves the public purse far more than it costs, and the young people involved gain experience that no lecture can offer.

Meanwhile, taking responsibility also sharpens young people's competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for their overall development and future success.

In conclusion, a sense of responsibility contributes not only to a better society but also to a stronger self. Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping the world we share.

**中文**
在社会快速发展的时代，增强青年的责任感至关重要。在当今快速变化的社会里，责任感不仅关乎个人成长，也关乎一个社区的健康。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出完成一件任务本身。

首先，责任感是一个健康社区的基石。通过一次次小小的援手，它建立起任何规矩都造不出来的信任。一个没人愿意搭把手的班级，很快就会变成一群坐在同一间屋子里的陌生人——这样的事并不少见。

其次，从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。志愿者付出的每一小时，为公共财政省下的远不止这一小时，参与的年轻人也获得了课堂给不了的经验。

同时，承担责任也能增强年轻人的竞争力，有助于为他们的全面发展和未来成功铺路。

总之，责任感不仅有助于更好的社会，也有助于更强的自己。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们共同的世界中举足轻重。

## 换题只改三处

| 改哪里 | 怎么改 |
|---|---|
| 第一段第 1 句 | 换成题目给定的句子，照抄 |
| 开头第 2 句的 X、结尾第 1 句的 X | 换成题目主题词 |
| 三段里的人 | 学生 / 父母 / 城市 / 工人 |

**其余整段照搬。** 四个类别共用同一个开头和结尾，只换一个名词。
