# 六维语料库（每维五类：分论点 / 说明 / 原因 / 举例 / 措施）

## 怎么用

每一维都是一个"抽屉"，里面按功能分好格子。写第二段时：

```
分论点 → 举例 → 说明        （一个论点写完整，约 45 词）
```

题目要你提建议时，再加：

```
措施 → 原因                （约 30 词，放在第二段最后一点）
```

**分论点只选两个不同维度**，不要两个都从同一维度拿。

## ① 个人成长维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> Such an ability can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success.
> 这样的能力能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。

**【分论点 B】**
> It lays a solid foundation for our long-term development, and the earlier it is built, the more it pays.
> 它为我们的长远发展打下扎实基础；而且建得越早，回报越多。

**【展开说明】**
> The same effort spent early saves far more time later, because skills build on each other instead of starting from zero.
> 同样一份力气，早花出去能省下后来更多时间，因为技能是一层层叠上去的，不是每次都从零开始。

**【原因】**
> Most people fail here not because they lack ability, but because they want results before they are willing to practise.
> 大多数人败在这里，不是因为没有能力，而是想在肯练之前先看到结果。

**【举例模板】**
> A student who ______ every day can ______ in a year, while a classmate who waits for ______ gets nothing.
> 一个每天______的学生，一年后能______；而等着______的同学什么也得不到。

**【措施】**
> It is highly necessary for schools to ______, because a habit formed early costs far less than a skill bought late.
> 学校极有必要______，因为早养成的习惯，比晚花钱买来的技能便宜得多。

## ② 人际社会维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> Such carelessness may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship.
> 这种草率会给我们的关系带来负面影响。

**【分论点 B】★讲义原句**
> In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally.
> 从长远看，它还可能对我们的身心健康构成挑战。

**【展开说明】**
> A relationship takes years to build and one sentence to damage, so the cost of carelessness is always higher than it looks.
> 一段关系要几年才能建立，却只要一句话就能毁掉，所以草率的代价总比看上去更高。

**【原因】**
> People act carelessly mainly because they cannot see the face of the person they are hurting.
> 人们之所以草率，主要是因为他们看不见被伤害那个人的脸。

**【举例模板】**
> A student who ______ loses the trust of ______, while one who ______ keeps it for years.
> 一个______的学生会失去______的信任；而一个______的人，能把这份信任守住很多年。

**【措施】**
> It is highly necessary for schools to teach ______ as a basic skill, because rules alone cannot replace a habit.
> 学校极有必要把______当作一项基本技能来教，因为光有规矩替代不了习惯。

## ③ 科技效率维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> We are at a point where mastering these tools is less of an advantage and more of a basic requirement for full participation in society.
> 我们已经到了这个节点：掌握这些工具不再算优势，而是充分参与社会的基本要求。

**【分论点 B】**
> The same tool that saves us an hour can quietly take away our judgement.
> 同一件工具能为我们省下一小时，也可能悄悄拿走我们的判断力。

**【展开说明】**
> Convenience is useful only when we still know what we are doing with it.
> 只有在我们还清楚自己拿它做什么的时候，便利才算有用。

**【原因】**
> Tools are designed to be easy, and easy things are rarely done with care.
> 工具生来就是为了省事，而省事的事情很少被认真对待。

**【举例模板】**
> A worker who ______ finishes in an hour what a colleague needs a week to do by hand.
> 会______的职员，一小时就能做完同事手工要一周的活。

**【措施】**
> It is highly necessary for schools to teach students to use tools rather than merely to operate them, because operation is a skill of the hand and use is a skill of the mind.
> 学校极有必要教学生去"用"工具，而不只是会"操作"工具，因为操作是手上的技能，使用是头脑的技能。

## ④ 经济现实维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> From a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option.
> 从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。

**【分论点 B】**
> In the long run, the price of ignoring it is far higher than the cost of preparing for it.
> 从长远看，忽视它的代价远高于为它做准备的代价。

**【展开说明】**
> What looks cheap today often sends its bill to someone else tomorrow.
> 今天看起来便宜的东西，往往把账单寄给了明天的别人。

**【原因】**
> People underestimate long-term cost because the bill never arrives on the same day as the decision.
> 人们之所以低估长期成本，是因为账单从来不在做决定的那天寄到。

**【举例模板】**
> A city that spends on ______ saves far more on ______ within ten years.
> 一个在______上花钱的城市，十年内在______上省下的要多得多。

**【措施】**
> It is highly necessary for governments to ______, because prevention is always cheaper than repair.
> 政府极有必要______，因为预防永远比修补便宜。

## ⑤ 环境可持续维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> Adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle is an imperative for safeguarding our future generations.
> 采取环保的生活方式，是保护子孙后代的当务之急。

**【分论点 B】**
> Resources are finite, and a habit built today decides what is left tomorrow.
> 资源是有限的，今天养成的习惯决定了明天还剩多少。

**【展开说明】**
> The earth does not send us an invoice; it simply keeps a record.
> 地球不会给我们寄账单，它只是默默记账。

**【原因】**
> People ignore environmental cost because it is spread across millions of people and dozens of years, so no single person feels responsible for it.
> 人们忽视环境成本，是因为它分摊在几百万人和几十年里，于是没有一个人觉得自己该负责。

**【举例模板】**
> A student who walks to school instead of taking a car saves more energy in a year than a single campaign can.
> 一个走路上学而不是坐车的学生，一年省下的能源超过任何一场宣传活动。

**【措施】**
> It is highly necessary for cities to keep green space within walking distance, because a habit needs a place to happen.
> 城市极有必要保证步行范围内有绿地，因为习惯需要一个发生的场所。

## ⑥ 知识思维维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> In the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views.
> 在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息与极端观点的第一道防线。

**【分论点 B】**
> Reading widely is what separates a person with opinions from a person with judgement.
> 广泛阅读，把"有观点的人"和"有判断力的人"区分开来。

**【展开说明】**
> A mind that never asks a second question is easy to lead anywhere.
> 一个从不追问第二个问题的头脑，很容易被带向任何地方。

**【原因】**
> Algorithms show us what we already agree with, so the habit of doubt has to be trained on purpose.
> 算法只给我们看自己本来就赞同的东西，所以"怀疑"这个习惯必须刻意训练。

**【举例模板】**
> A reader who checks the source before sharing is rarely fooled, while one who trusts the first result is led without noticing.
> 转发前核实来源的读者很少被骗；而相信第一条结果的人，往往在不知不觉中被带偏。

**【措施】**
> It is highly necessary for schools to teach students how to trace a claim to its source, because a habit formed early is hard to break.
> 学校极有必要教学生把一个说法追溯到它的源头，因为早年养成的习惯很难改掉。

## 五类句子的分工

| 功能 | 放在哪 | 作用 | 中文 | 优先级 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 分论点 | 每点第一句 | 亮观点，最容易拿分 | 每点的核心主张 | ★★★ |
| 举例 | 分论点后 | 证明"我真想过" | 一个具体的人做了什么 | ★★★ |
| 说明 | 例子后 | 把例子和观点连起来 | 为什么会这样 | ★★ |
| 原因 | 想加深度时 | 解释成因，冲高分用 | 背后机制 | ★ |
| 措施 | 题目要建议时 | 放第二段最后一点 | 应该怎么办 | ★★ |

**只有时间背两类的话，背【分论点】和【举例模板】。** 这两类就能撑起一篇 200 词的文章。

# 六、四类范文（全部由上面语料拼成）

## A · 个人成长类（① ⑥ ④）· 自律

Nowadays, more and more students have realized the importance of self-discipline in their personal growth. Few would deny the value of self-discipline, yet far fewer put that belief into practice, because knowing and doing are two very different things.

To begin with, self-discipline can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success. A student who writes five hundred words every morning finishes a thesis in a semester, while a classmate who waits for inspiration gets nothing. The same effort spent early saves far more time later, because skills build on each other instead of starting from zero.

Moreover, reading widely is what separates a person with opinions from a person with judgement, and self-discipline is what makes the reading happen. A mind that never asks a second question is easy to lead anywhere, while one trained to doubt is hard to fool.

Finally, from a pragmatic standpoint, self-discipline is a cost effective and sustainable option. In the long run, the price of ignoring it is far higher than the cost of preparing for it. A decision that looks cheap today often sends its bill to someone else tomorrow.

In short, self-discipline matters more than we usually admit. It is not something we learn once and then forget, but a habit we build a little every day, and the sooner we begin, the less we will regret later in life.

**中文**
如今越来越多的学生意识到自律在个人成长中的重要性。很少有人会否认自律的价值，但真正付诸行动的人要少得多，因为知道和做到是两件不同的事。

首先，自律能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。一个每天早上写五百字的学生，一学期就能写完一篇论文；而等着灵感的同学什么也得不到。同样一份力气，早花出去能省下后来更多时间，因为技能是一层层叠上去的，不是每次都从零开始。

其次，广泛阅读把"有观点的人"和"有判断力的人"区分开来，而自律正是让阅读真正发生的东西。一个从不追问第二个问题的头脑，很容易被带向任何地方；而被训练过怀疑的头脑，很难被骗。

最后，从务实的角度看，自律是一个划算且可持续的选择。从长远看，忽视它的代价远高于为它做准备的代价。今天看起来便宜的决定，往往把账单寄给了明天的别人。

总之，自律的意义比我们通常承认的更大。它不是学一次就忘的东西，而是每天一点点建起来的习惯；越早开始，将来后悔的越少。

## B · 环境保护类（⑤ ① ④）· 热爱自然

With the trend of urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for nature. Few would deny the value of a love for nature, yet far fewer put that belief into practice, because knowing and doing are two very different things.

To begin with, adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle is an imperative for safeguarding our future generations. A student who walks to school instead of taking a car saves more energy in a year than a single campaign can. The earth does not send us an invoice; it simply keeps a record.

Moreover, contact with nature can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success. A young person who has watched a river change across four seasons learns patience that no classroom can teach, and that patience outlasts most of what we memorise for exams.

Finally, from a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option. Cities that keep green space spend less on health care and attract more talent than those that do not. What looks cheap today often sends its bill to someone else tomorrow.

In short, a love for nature matters more than we usually admit. It is not something we learn once and then forget, but a habit we build a little every day, and the sooner we begin, the less we will regret later in life.

**中文**
随着城镇化推进，城市居民培养对自然的更深热爱至关重要。很少有人会否认热爱自然的价值，但真正付诸行动的人要少得多，因为知道和做到是两件不同的事。

首先，采取环保的生活方式，是保护子孙后代的当务之急。每天走路上学而不是坐车的学生，一年省下的能源超过任何一场宣传活动。地球不会给我们寄账单，它只是默默记账。

其次，亲近自然能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。一个看着一条河走过四季的年轻人，学到的是课堂教不出来的耐心，而这份耐心比我们为考试背下的东西活得更久。

最后，从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。保留绿地的城市在医疗上花费更少，吸引的人才更多。今天看起来便宜的东西，往往把账单寄给了明天的别人。

总之，热爱自然的意义比我们通常承认的更大。它不是学一次就忘的东西，而是每天一点点建起来的习惯；越早开始，将来后悔的越少。

## C · 科技数字化类（③ ⑥ ②）· 数字素养

There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and skills in today's world. Few would deny the value of digital literacy, yet far fewer put that belief into practice, because knowing and doing are two very different things.

To begin with, we are at a point where mastering these tools is less of an advantage and more of a basic requirement for full participation in society. A worker who can clean a spreadsheet and write a formula finishes in an hour what a colleague needs a week to do by hand. Tools are designed to be easy, and easy things are rarely done with care.

Moreover, in the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views. A reader who checks the source before sharing is rarely fooled, while one who trusts the first result is led without noticing.

Finally, careless use may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship. In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally. A single careless comment online can damage a friendship that took years to build.

In short, digital literacy matters more than we usually admit. It is not something we learn once and then forget, but a habit we build a little every day, and the sooner we begin, the less we will regret later in life.

**中文**
当今世界，人们越来越意识到数字素养与技能的重要性。很少有人会否认数字素养的价值，但真正付诸行动的人要少得多，因为知道和做到是两件不同的事。

首先，我们已经到了这个节点：掌握这些工具不再算优势，而是充分参与社会的基本要求。会清洗表格、会写公式的职员，一小时就能做完同事手工要一周的活。工具生来就是为了省事，而省事的事情很少被认真对待。

其次，在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息与极端观点的第一道防线。转发前核实来源的读者很少被骗；而相信第一条结果的人，往往在不知不觉中被带偏。

最后，草率使用会给我们的关系带来负面影响；从长远看，还可能危害身心健康。网络上一条轻率的评论，就能毁掉一段花了几年建立起来的友谊。

总之，数字素养的意义比我们通常承认的更大。它不是学一次就忘的东西，而是每天一点点建起来的习惯；越早开始，将来后悔的越少。

## D · 社会类（② ④ ①）· 责任感

In an era of rapid social development, it is vitally important to enhance young people's sense of responsibility. Few would deny the value of a sense of responsibility, yet far fewer put that belief into practice, because knowing and doing are two very different things.

To begin with, the absence of such a habit may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship. In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally. A class where nobody is willing to help out soon becomes a group of strangers sitting in the same room.

Moreover, from a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option. Every hour a volunteer gives saves the public purse far more. People underestimate long-term cost because the bill never arrives on the same day as the decision.

Finally, taking responsibility can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success. A student trusted with a real task hesitates less when the community needs a hand, and that confidence follows him into every job he takes.

In short, a sense of responsibility matters more than we usually admit. It is not something we learn once and then forget, but a habit we build a little every day, and the sooner we begin, the less we will regret later in life.

**中文**
在社会快速发展的时代，增强青年的责任感至关重要。很少有人会否认责任感的价值，但真正付诸行动的人要少得多，因为知道和做到是两件不同的事。

首先，缺少这样的习惯会给我们的关系带来负面影响；从长远看，还可能危害身心健康。一个没人愿意搭把手的班级，很快就会变成一群坐在同一间屋子里的陌生人。

其次，从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。志愿者付出的每一小时，为公共财政省下的远不止这一小时。人们之所以低估长期成本，是因为账单从来不在做决定的那天寄到。

最后，承担责任能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。被托付过真任务的学生，在社区需要帮忙时更少犹豫，而这份自信会跟着他走进之后每一份工作。

总之，责任感的意义比我们通常承认的更大。它不是学一次就忘的东西，而是每天一点点建起来的习惯；越早开始，将来后悔的越少。

## 换题只改三处

| 改哪里 | 怎么改 |
|---|---|
| 第一段第 1 句 | 换成题目给定的句子（照抄） |
| 三段的 X | 换成题目主题词 |
| 举例句里的人 | 换成学生 / 父母 / 城市 / 工人 |
