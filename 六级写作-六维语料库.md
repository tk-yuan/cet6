# 六维语料库（讲义语言 + 六维论点 + 四类范文）

## 一、怎么用

每一维是一个抽屉，按功能分格。写第二段：

```
分论点 → 举例 → 说明/原因        （一个论点约 50 词）
```

题目要建议时，加一格：

```
措施                             （放第二段最后一点）
```

**两三个分论点必须取自不同维度**，不要都从一个维度拿。

## 二、六维语料库

### ① 个人成长维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> Such an ability can sharpen our competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for our overall development and future success.
> 这样的能力能增强我们的竞争力，有助于为全面发展和未来成功铺路。

**【分论点 B】**
> It lays a solid foundation for our long-term development, and the earlier it is built, the more it pays.
> 它为长远发展打下扎实基础；建得越早，回报越多。

**【展开说明】**
> The same effort spent early saves far more time later, because skills build on each other instead of starting from zero.
> 同样一份力气，早花出去能省下后来更多时间，因为技能是一层层叠上去的，不是每次都从零开始。

**【原因】★讲义句式**
> The main contributing factor toward this gap is that people want results before they are willing to practise.
> 造成这一差距的主要因素是：人们想在肯练之前先看到结果。

> 备用：It is related to the fact that the first months of any new skill feel slow and unrewarding.
> 这与这样一个事实有关：任何新技能的头几个月，都显得缓慢又没有回报。

**【举例模板】**
> A good case in point is a student who ______ every day; within a year he can ______, while a classmate who waits for ______ gets nothing.
> 一个典型例子是：一个每天______的学生，一年内就能______；而等着______的同学什么也得不到。

**【措施】★讲义句式**
> It is highly necessary for schools to ______, because a habit formed early costs far less than a skill bought late.
> 学校极有必要______，因为早养成的习惯，比晚买的技能便宜得多。

### ② 人际社会维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> Such carelessness may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship.
> 这种草率会给我们的关系带来负面影响。

**【分论点 B】★讲义原句**
> In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally.
> 从长远看，它还可能对我们的身心健康构成挑战。

**【展开说明】**
> A relationship takes years to build and one sentence to damage, so the cost of carelessness is always higher than it looks.
> 一段关系要几年才能建立，却只要一句话就能毁掉，所以草率的代价总比看上去更高。

**【原因】★讲义句式**
> It is related to the fact that people cannot see the face of the person they are hurting.
> 这与这样一个事实有关：人们看不见被自己伤害那个人的脸。

**【举例模板】**
> A good case in point is a student who ______ and loses the trust of ______, while one who ______ keeps it for years.
> 一个典型例子是：一个______的学生失去了______的信任；而一个______的人，把这份信任守了很多年。

**【措施】★讲义句式**
> It is highly necessary for schools to teach ______ as a basic skill, because rules alone cannot replace a habit.
> 学校极有必要把______当作基本技能来教，因为光有规矩替代不了习惯。

### ③ 科技效率维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> We are at a point where mastering these tools is less of an advantage and more of a basic requirement for full participation in society.
> 我们已经到了这个节点：掌握这些工具不再算优势，而是充分参与社会的基本要求。

**【分论点 B】**
> The same tool that saves us an hour can quietly take away our judgement.
> 同一件工具能为我们省下一小时，也可能悄悄拿走我们的判断力。

**【展开说明】**
> Convenience is useful only when we still know what we are doing with it.
> 只有在我们还清楚自己拿它做什么的时候，便利才算有用。

**【原因】★讲义句式**
> The main contributing factor toward this tendency is that tools are designed to be easy, and easy things are rarely done with care.
> 造成这一倾向的主要因素是：工具生来就是为了省事，而省事的事情很少被认真对待。

**【举例模板】**
> A good case in point is a worker who ______ and finishes in an hour what a colleague needs a week to do by hand.
> 一个典型例子是：会______的工人，一小时就做完同事手工要一周的活。

**【措施】★讲义句式**
> It is highly necessary for schools to teach students to use tools rather than merely to operate them, because operation is a skill of the hand and use is a skill of the mind.
> 学校极有必要教学生去"用"工具，而不只是会"操作"，因为操作是手上的技能，使用是头脑的技能。

### ④ 经济现实维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> From a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option.
> 从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。

**【分论点 B】**
> In the long run, the price of ignoring it is far higher than the cost of preparing for it.
> 从长远看，忽视它的代价远高于为它做准备的代价。

**【展开说明】**
> What looks cheap today often sends its bill to someone else tomorrow.
> 今天看起来便宜的东西，往往把账单寄给了明天的别人。

**【原因】★讲义句式**
> It is related to the fact that the bill never arrives on the same day as the decision, so the cost is easy to ignore.
> 这与这样一个事实有关：账单从来不在做决定的那天寄到，所以成本很容易被忽视。

**【举例模板】**
> A good case in point is a city that spends on ______ and saves far more on ______ within ten years.
> 一个典型例子是：在______上投钱的城市，十年内在______上省下的要多得多。

**【措施】★讲义句式**
> It is highly necessary for governments to ______, because prevention is always cheaper than repair.
> 政府极有必要______，因为预防永远比修补便宜。

---

### ⑤ 环境可持续维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> Adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle is an imperative for safeguarding our future generations.
> 采取环保的生活方式，是保护子孙后代的当务之急。

**【分论点 B】**
> Resources are finite, and a habit built today decides what is left tomorrow.
> 资源有限，今天养成的习惯决定了明天还剩多少。

**【展开说明】**
> The earth does not send us an invoice; it simply keeps a record.
> 地球不会给我们寄账单，它只是默默记账。

**【原因】★讲义句式**
> It is related to the fact that environmental costs are spread across millions of people and dozens of years, so no single person feels responsible for them.
> 这与这样一个事实有关：环境成本分摊在几百万人和几十年里，于是没有一个人觉得自己该负责。

**【举例模板】**
> A good case in point is a student who walks to school instead of taking a car and saves more energy in a year than a single campaign can.
> 一个典型例子是：走路上学而不是坐车的学生，一年省下的能源超过任何一场宣传活动。

**【措施】★讲义句式**
> It is highly necessary for cities to keep green space within walking distance, because a habit needs a place to happen.
> 城市极有必要保证步行范围内有绿地，因为习惯需要一个发生的场所。

### ⑥ 知识思维维度

**【分论点 A】★讲义原句**
> In the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views.
> 在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息与极端观点的第一道防线。

**【分论点 B】**
> Reading widely is what separates a person with opinions from a person with judgement.
> 广泛阅读，把"有观点的人"和"有判断力的人"区分开来。

**【展开说明】**
> A mind that never asks a second question is easy to lead anywhere.
> 一个从不追问第二个问题的头脑，很容易被带向任何地方。

**【原因】★讲义句式**
> The main contributing factor toward this weakness is that algorithms keep showing us what we already agree with.
> 造成这一弱点的主要因素是：算法不断给我们看自己本来就已经赞同的东西。

**【举例模板】**
> A good case in point is a reader who checks the source before sharing and is rarely fooled, while one who trusts the first result is led without noticing.
> 一个典型例子是：转发前核实来源的读者很少被骗；而相信第一条结果的人，往往在不知不觉中被带偏。

**【措施】★讲义句式**
> It is highly necessary for schools to teach students how to trace a claim to its source, because a habit formed early is hard to break.
> 学校极有必要教学生把一个说法追到源头，因为早年养成的习惯很难改掉。

## 四、八种论证方法 + 建议句式

### 论证方法（每条理由后面挂一个）

| 方法 | 句式 | 中文 |

| 反证 | With / Without sth., sb. would… | 有了／没有它，某人就会…… |
| 条件 | If / When + 从句（主将从现、虚拟语气） | 如果／当…… |

| 影响 | 主题词 is beneficial / harmful to… | 有利于／不利于 |

## 五、四类范文

## A · 个人成长类（① ⑥ ④）· 自律

Nowadays, more and more students have realized the importance of self-discipline in their personal growth. In today's fast-changing society, self-discipline plays a vital role not only in academic success but also in the kind of adult we become. From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond the classroom.

To begin with, self-discipline serves as a cornerstone for students' personal growth and long-term success. Through small decisions repeated every day, it helps young people cultivate resilience, patience and a proactive mindset, all of which lay a solid foundation for future achievement. A good case in point is a student who writes five hundred words every morning; within a semester he finishes a thesis, while a classmate who waits for inspiration writes nothing.

Moreover, self-discipline equips students with the habit of thinking for themselves. In an age of information overload, the ability to judge independently is indispensable. By choosing what to read and when to stop, students broaden their horizons and form their own judgement.

Meanwhile, from a pragmatic standpoint, self-discipline is a cost effective and sustainable option. It is related to the fact that the bill never arrives on the same day as the decision, so the cost of laziness is easy to ignore until it is too late.

In conclusion, self-discipline contributes not only to academic excellence but also to character formation. Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping our overall development.

**中文**
如今越来越多的学生意识到自律在个人成长中的重要性。在当今快速变化的社会里，自律不仅在学业上重要，也决定我们会长成什么样的成年人。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出课堂。

首先，自律是学生个人成长与长期成功的一块基石。通过每天重复的小决定，它帮助年轻人培养韧性、耐心和主动的心态，这一切为未来的成就打下扎实基础。一个典型例子是：一个每天早上写五百字的学生，一学期就能写完一篇论文；而等着灵感的同学什么也写不出来。

其次，自律让人养成独立思考的习惯。在信息过载的时代，独立判断的能力不可或缺。通过选择读什么、什么时候停下，学生拓宽了视野，形成了自己的判断。

同时，从务实的角度看，自律是一个划算且可持续的选择。这与一个事实有关：账单从来不在做决定的那天寄到，所以懒惰的代价很容易被忽视，直到为时已晚。

总之，自律不仅有助于学业优秀，也有助于品格形成。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们的整体发展中举足轻重。

## B · 环境保护类（⑤ ① ④）· 热爱自然

With the trend of urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for nature. In today's fast-changing society, a love for nature plays a vital role not only in our physical health but also in the kind of city we build. From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond a weekend trip.

To begin with, adopting an environmentally friendly lifestyle is an imperative for safeguarding our future generations. Through small daily choices, it helps families cut waste and save energy, all of which lay a solid foundation for a livable city. A good case in point is a student who walks to school; in a year he saves more energy than a single campaign can.

Moreover, contact with nature equips young people with patience and curiosity. In an age of screens, the ability to observe something slowly is indispensable. By watching a river change across four seasons, students learn a kind of attention that no classroom can teach.

Meanwhile, from a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option. It is related to the fact that environmental costs are spread across millions of people and dozens of years, so no single person feels responsible for them.

In conclusion, a love for nature contributes not only to a healthier body but also to a saner mind. Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping the world we leave behind.

**中文**
随着城镇化推进，城市居民培养对自然的更深热爱至关重要。在当今快速变化的社会里，热爱自然不仅关乎身体健康，也关乎我们会建成什么样的城市。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出一个周末的郊游。

首先，采取环保的生活方式，是保护子孙后代的当务之急。通过每天的小选择，它帮助家庭减少浪费、节约能源，这一切为一座宜居的城市打下扎实基础。一个典型例子是：一个走路上学的学生，一年省下的能源超过任何一场宣传活动。

其次，亲近自然让年轻人拥有耐心和好奇。在一个被屏幕包围的时代，慢慢观察一件事物的能力不可或缺。通过看着一条河走过四季，学生学到一种课堂教不出来的专注。

同时，从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。这与一个事实有关：环境成本分摊在几百万人和几十年里，于是没有一个人觉得自己该负责。

总之，热爱自然不仅有助于更健康的身体，也有助于更清醒的心智。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们留给后代的世界中举足轻重。

## C · 科技数字化类（③ ⑥ ②）· 数字素养

There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and skills in today's world. In today's fast-changing society, digital literacy plays a vital role not only in finding a job but also in telling truth from noise. From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond operating a device.

To begin with, we are at a point where mastering these tools is less of an advantage and more of a basic requirement for full participation in society. Through daily use, it helps workers finish in an hour what once took a week, which lays a solid foundation for a productive career. A good case in point is a clerk who can write a formula; he is the one trusted with the work that really matters.

Moreover, in the age of social media algorithms, the ability to think critically is our first line of defense against misinformation and polarized views. The main contributing factor toward this weakness is that algorithms keep showing us what we already agree with.

Meanwhile, careless use may have negative implications in our interpersonal relationship. In the long run, it may pose a challenge to our health both physically and mentally.

In conclusion, digital literacy contributes not only to efficiency but also to sound judgement. Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping how we live and work.

**中文**
当今世界，人们越来越意识到数字素养与技能的重要性。在当今快速变化的社会里，数字素养不仅在求职时重要，也关乎能否分清真相与噪音。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出会操作一台设备。

首先，我们已经到了这个节点：掌握这些工具不再算优势，而是充分参与社会的基本要求。通过日常使用，它让工人一小时做完过去要一周的活，这为一份高效的职业生涯打下扎实基础。一个典型例子是：会写公式的职员，才是被托付真正重要工作的人。

其次，在社交媒体算法盛行的时代，批判性思维是我们抵御错误信息与极端观点的第一道防线。造成这一弱点的主要因素是：算法不断给我们看自己本来就已经赞同的东西。

同时，草率使用会给我们的关系带来负面影响；从长远看，还可能危害身心健康。

总之，数字素养不仅有助于效率，也有助于清醒的判断。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们如何生活与工作中举足轻重。

## D · 社会类（② ④ ①）· 责任感

In an era of rapid social development, it is vitally important to enhance young people's sense of responsibility. In today's fast-changing society, a sense of responsibility plays a vital role not only in personal growth but also in the health of a community. From where I stand, its value often extends far beyond a single task.

To begin with, a sense of responsibility serves as a cornerstone for a healthy community. Through small acts of help, it builds a trust that no rule can create. A good case in point is a class where nobody is willing to help out; within a term it becomes a group of strangers sitting in the same room.

Moreover, from a pragmatic standpoint, it is a cost effective and sustainable option. Every hour a volunteer gives saves the public purse far more than it costs, and the young people involved gain experience that no lecture can offer.

Meanwhile, taking responsibility also sharpens young people's competitive edge, which is conducive to paving the way for their overall development and future success.

In conclusion, a sense of responsibility contributes not only to a better society but also to a stronger self. Its influence, whether direct or subtle, carries significant weight in shaping the world we share.

**中文**
在社会快速发展的时代，增强青年的责任感至关重要。在当今快速变化的社会里，责任感不仅关乎个人成长，也关乎一个社区的健康。在我看来，它的价值往往远远超出完成一件任务本身。

首先，责任感是一个健康社区的基石。通过一次次小小的援手，它建立起任何规矩都造不出来的信任。一个典型例子是：一个没人愿意搭把手的班级，一学期之内就变成一群坐在同一间屋子里的陌生人。

其次，从务实的角度看，这是一个划算且可持续的选择。志愿者付出的每一小时，为公共财政省下的远不止这一小时，参与的年轻人也获得了课堂给不了的经验。

同时，承担责任也能增强年轻人的竞争力，有助于为他们的全面发展和未来成功铺路。

总之，责任感不仅有助于更好的社会，也有助于更强的自己。它的影响无论直接还是微妙，都在塑造我们共同的世界中举足轻重。
