# 六级写作 · 套用麦粒素模板（A卷：2021–2023 共 12 篇）

> **范围说明**：文件夹里只有 2021.6–2026.6 共 9 场 27 套，**没有 2020 和 2022**，所以没有那两年的范文。
> **词数**：每篇 230–250 词。注意题目原文写的是 *no more than 200 words*，超字数在个别考场有扣分风险，是否这样写你自己权衡。

## 删论点怎么删（考场调词数用）

第二段现在是三个独立论点，各有标记，**整句整段删掉不影响其余**：

```
主题句
First, in terms of …        论点一：角度 + 举例     ← 必留
Second, in contrast to …    论点二：正反对照        ← 可删
Third, without …            论点三：反证            ← 先删这个
```

| 写几点 | 删什么 | 大约词数 |
|---|---|---|
| 3 点 | 不删 | 230–250 |
| 2 点 | 删掉 **Third** 那一句 | 180–195 |
| 1 点 | 再删掉 **Second** 那一句 | 155–170 |

**规则**：只能从后往前删。删了 Third 再删 Second，中间不能跳过——因为 Second 和 Third 都是对 First 的补充，删掉 First 段落就没有论点了。

**注意**：`Without…` 那句删掉后，前面一句必须是完整句（以句号结尾）。所有范文都满足，你改写时别把两句合成一句。

## 模板原句（他的，一字未改）

**第一段三句**
```
① 背景：There is a growing awareness / recognition of …
② 反问：Could you imagine how utterly distinct our world would be with / without …?
③ 立场：Admittedly, … is rewarding in a way, but personally I am inclined to
        take a dim view of …          ← 负面话题
        Admittedly, … is a complicated and lengthy process, but the
        achievements often bring tremendous social and economic benefits.  ← 正面话题
```

**第二段主题句（四选一）**
```
好处：The pluses associated with this topic are numerous and substantial,
      yielding profound implications if widely adopted and sustainable over the long run.
弊端：The deficiencies associated with this topic are numerous and substantial,
      posing profound challenges if widely adopted and sustainable over the long run.
```

**第三段建议**
```
It is highly necessary for sb. to do sth.
It would do better if sb. did sth.
It is high time that sb. did sth.
```

## 2021年6月 第1套 · 图表·城镇化（正面）

As is depicted in the chart, the urban population of China has risen sharply over the past decades. Could you imagine how utterly distinct our living conditions would be without such a movement of people from the countryside? Admittedly, urbanization is a complicated and lengthy process, but the achievements often bring tremendous social and economic benefits.

The pluses associated with this topic are numerous and substantial, yielding profound implications if widely adopted and sustainable over the long run. First, in terms of career prospects, cities gather jobs that a village can never offer. A good case in point is a young graduate from a small county who finds a decent job in a provincial capital within a week, while his cousin in the village waits a whole year for seasonal work. Second, in contrast to rural life, city life also provides better schools and hospitals, although it may cost more and move faster. Third, without this flow of people, factories would stand empty and students would study in crowded village classrooms.

It is highly necessary for local governments to build public services in smaller cities as well. It would do better if people could prosper without crowding into a few metropolises, and if old neighbourhoods were protected instead of pulled down.

**译文**

如图所示，过去几十年中国的城镇人口大幅上升。如果没有这场从乡村到城市的人口流动，我们的生活条件会多么不同？诚然，城镇化是一个复杂而漫长的过程，但它带来的成就往往会产生巨大的社会和经济效益。

与本主题相关的好处众多且重大，若被广泛采用并长期持续，将产生深远影响。就职业前途而言，城市聚集了乡村永远无法提供的岗位。一个典型例子是：小县城毕业的年轻人一周内就能在省城找到一份体面工作，而他村里的表弟却要等整整一年才有一份季节性零工。与乡村生活相比，城市还能提供更好的学校和医院，尽管代价更高、节奏更快。没有这样的人口流动，工厂会空着，学生要在拥挤的村小里上课。

地方政府同样有必要在小城市建公共服务。如果人们不必挤进少数几个大城市也能过上好日子，如果老城区是被保护而不是被推倒，情况会更好。

Could you imagine how utterly distinct a family's life would be without this change? Second, in contrast to aid given as cash alone, help that builds a road or a clinic keeps working after the officials leave. Third, without such capacity, one bad harvest would undo a decade of effort.

与本主题相关的好处众多且重大，若被广泛采用并长期持续，将产生深远影响。就个人能力而言，过去什么都卖不出去的一户人家，现在能经营小生意、自己记账。一个典型例子是：一个山村因为修了新路，冷链车开进来了，过去烂在树上的水果现在能卖到三个省。与单纯发现金相比，修一条路、建一间卫生室的帮助在干部离开后仍在起作用。没有这样的能力，一场歉收就会抵消十年的努力。

地方政府极有必要用同一个问题检验每个项目：它能不能自己转起来？如果培养年轻人去经营这些产业，而不是仅仅把产业交到他们手上，情况会更好。

## 2021年12月 第1套 · 现象·网络虚假信息（负面）

There is a growing awareness of the problem that young people cannot tell real news from fabricated news online. Could you imagine how utterly distinct our public discussion would be with so many rumours spreading unchecked every day? Admittedly, reading news online is rewarding in a way, but personally I am inclined to take a dim view of sharing it without a second thought.

The deficiencies associated with this topic are numerous and substantial, posing profound challenges if widely adopted and sustainable over the long run. First, in terms of personal abilities, a student who never checks a source gradually loses the habit of judging. A good case in point is a rumour about a stranger that travelled through a hundred group chats in one hour, while the correction reached almost nobody. Second, in contrast to a careful reader, a careless one becomes a tool of whoever wrote the lie. Third, without basic media literacy, even a well-educated person can be led in the wrong direction for years.

It is highly necessary for schools to teach students how to trace a piece of news to its origin. It would do better if platforms were obliged to mark unverified content clearly.

**译文**

人们越来越意识到这样一个问题：年轻人分不清网络上的真新闻和编造新闻。如果每天有这么多谣言不受核查地传播，我们的公共讨论会多么不同？诚然，在网上读新闻在某种程度上有好处，但我个人倾向于对不加思索地转发持否定态度。

与本主题相关的弊端众多且重大，若被广泛采用并长期持续，将带来严峻挑战。就个人能力而言，从不核实来源的学生会逐渐失去判断的习惯。一个典型例子是：关于一个陌生人的谣言一小时传遍上百个群，而辟谣几乎没人看到。与谨慎的读者相比，草率的读者会成为造谣者手里的工具。没有基本的媒介素养，即使受过良好教育的人也可能被误导多年。

学校极有必要教学生把一条新闻追到源头。如果平台被要求明确标注未经核实的内容，情况会更好。

There is a growing awareness of the phenomenon that star chasing is prevalent among teenagers. Could you imagine how utterly distinct a young person's values would be with so much time and money spent on idols? Second, in contrast to a well-chosen model, a model chosen for looks or scandal teaches that attention is the only prize. Third, without guidance, admiration turns into imitation, and imitation into an empty life.

人们越来越意识到这样一个现象：追星在青少年中很普遍。如果这么多时间和金钱花在偶像身上，一个年轻人的价值观会多么不同？诚然，欣赏一个成功的人在某种程度上有好处，但我个人倾向于对不惜代价追星持否定态度。

与本主题相关的弊端众多且重大，若被广泛采用并长期持续，将带来严峻挑战。就个人品质而言，青少年选择的榜样会悄悄决定他三十岁时看重什么。一个典型例子是：有学生花掉一个月生活费去看演唱会，却说不出自己国家任何一位科学家的名字。与选对的榜样相比，凭长相或丑闻选出的榜样只教会人"注意力才是唯一的奖品"。没有引导，崇拜会变成模仿，模仿会变成空洞的人生。

父母和老师极有必要和青少年讨论偶像，而不是简单地禁止。如果学校请真正的专业人士来讲他们的工作，情况会更好。

## 2021年12月 第3套 · 现象·过度保护（负面）

There is a growing awareness of the phenomenon that some parents plan every hour of their children's lives. Could you imagine how utterly distinct a young adult's first year at university would be with such protection removed all at once? Admittedly, protecting a child is rewarding in a way, but personally I am inclined to take a dim view of removing every obstacle in advance.

The deficiencies associated with this topic are numerous and substantial, posing profound challenges if widely adopted and sustainable over the long run. First, in terms of personal abilities, competence is built from small failures rather than from perfect arrangements. A good case in point is a student who has never chosen a course alone and freezes on the first day of registration. Second, in contrast to a child allowed to pack his own bag and forget it once, one who is shielded from every risk arrives at adulthood unable to decide anything. Third, without practice, independence does not appear at eighteen; it has to be built from seven.

It is highly necessary for parents to hand over one decision a year. It would do better if they tolerated small mistakes while the cost of those mistakes is still low.

**译文**

人们越来越意识到这样一个现象：有些父母把孩子每一个小时都安排好。如果这样的保护一下子全部撤掉，一个年轻人大学第一年会多么不同？诚然，保护孩子在某种程度上有好处，但我个人倾向于对提前扫清一切障碍持否定态度。

与本主题相关的弊端众多且重大，若被广泛采用并长期持续，将带来严峻挑战。就个人能力而言，能力是从一次次小失败里长出来的，而不是从完美的安排里长出来的。一个典型例子是：从没独自选过课的学生，在选课系统开放第一天就僵住了。与那个被允许自己收拾书包、忘带一次的孩子相比，被挡掉一切风险的孩子到了成年什么都决定不了。没有练习，独立不会在十八岁那天自动出现；它要从七岁开始建。

父母极有必要每年交出一个决定权。如果他们能在犯错的代价还小的时候容忍小错，情况会更好。

## 2023年6月 第1套 · 心理健康（负面）

Today there is a growing awareness that mental well-being needs to be given as much attention as physical health. Could you imagine how utterly distinct a student's four years would be with so many sleepless nights and unspoken fears? Admittedly, paying attention to mental health is rewarding in a way, but personally I am inclined to take a dim view of treating exhaustion as weakness.

The deficiencies associated with this topic are numerous and substantial, posing profound challenges if widely adopted and sustainable over the long run. First, from the perspective of mental health, the body reports pain and the mind often does not. A good case in point is a student who keeps attending classes, answering messages and smiling, while sleep shrinks to four hours a night and concentration slips away. Second, in contrast to a broken arm, a broken month is invisible, and the damage is not noticed until an exam or a deadline breaks it open. Third, without early help, a few bad weeks can turn into a few hard years.

It is high time that universities made counselling free, anonymous and open at night. It would do better if asking for help cost less than hiding.

**译文**

如今人们越来越意识到，心理健康需要得到和身体健康同等的重视。如果伴随着这么多失眠的夜晚和说不出口的恐惧，一个学生的四年会多么不同？诚然，重视心理健康在某种程度上有好处，但我个人倾向于对把疲惫当成软弱持否定态度。

与本主题相关的弊端众多且重大，若被广泛采用并长期持续，将带来严峻挑战。从心理健康的角度看，身体会喊疼，心理往往不会。一个典型例子是：学生照常上课、回消息、还在笑，而睡眠已经缩到每晚四小时，注意力悄悄溜走。与一条断臂相比，一个崩溃的月份是看不见的，直到考试或截止日期把它撕开才被发现。没有早期的帮助，几个糟糕的星期会变成几年难熬的日子。

高校早该让心理咨询免费、匿名、夜里也开放。如果求助的代价比硬撑更低，情况会更好。

## 2023年6月 第2套 · 不同意见（正面）

When faced with differing opinions, we should try to reach agreement through friendly discussion and reasonable argument. Could you imagine how utterly distinct a team's decisions would be without any open disagreement? Admittedly, friendly discussion is a complicated and lengthy process, but the achievements often bring tremendous social and economic benefits.

The pluses associated with this topic are numerous and substantial, yielding profound implications if widely adopted and sustainable over the long run. First, in terms of personal abilities, arguing well forces a person to test his own reasons before offering them. A good case in point is a group that spends twenty minutes arguing over a plan and finds the flaw one member had already seen. Second, in contrast to a meeting where everyone nods, a meeting with one honest objection saves a month of redoing the work. Third, without such habits, the loudest voice wins and the quietest person stops contributing.

It is highly necessary for us to separate the person from the position. It would do better if we judged an idea by its reasons rather than by who offered it.

**译文**

面对不同意见时，我们应该通过友好讨论和理性辩论达成一致。如果没有任何公开的分歧，一个团队的决策会多么不同？诚然，友好讨论是一个复杂而漫长的过程，但它带来的成就往往会产生巨大的社会和经济效益。

与本主题相关的好处众多且重大，若被广泛采用并长期持续，将产生深远影响。就个人能力而言，善于争论会迫使一个人在开口前先检验自己的理由。一个典型例子是：一个团队为方案争论二十分钟，找出了某位成员早就看见的漏洞。与人人点头的会议相比，有一句诚实反对的会议能省下一个月的返工。没有这样的习惯，嗓门大的赢，最安静的人不再开口。

我们极有必要把人和观点分开。如果我们按理由而不是按提出者来评判一个想法，情况会更好。

每门课极有必要安排一个必须学生自己想办法完成的任务。如果考试考的是学生怎么学，而不只是记住了什么，情况会更好。

## 2023年12月 第1套 · 基础知识（正面）

As is known to all, gaining a sound knowledge of the basics is of vital importance for students to master an academic subject. Could you imagine how utterly distinct a third-year student's understanding would be without a firm foundation? Admittedly, learning the basics is a complicated and lengthy process, but the achievements often bring tremendous social and economic benefits.

The pluses associated with this topic are numerous and substantial, yielding profound implications if widely adopted and sustainable over the long run. **译文**

众所周知，扎实掌握基础知识对学生掌握一门学科至关重要。如果没有稳固的地基，一个大三学生的理解会多么不同？诚然，打基础是一个复杂而漫长的过程，但它带来的成就往往会产生巨大的社会和经济效益。

与本主题相关的好处众多且重大，若被广泛采用并长期持续，将产生深远影响。就个人能力而言，后面的每一个想法都挂在基础之上。一个典型例子是：真正弄懂"导数为什么衡量变化"的学生，不用笔记也能听懂经济学课；而只背公式的人把公式抄下来，什么也没懂。与没有深度的速度相比，扎实的基础让第三年更轻松而不是更艰难。没有它，每一章新内容都变成了猜。

学生极有必要在第一章多花一周。如果老师在开头放慢速度，而不是赶着讲完大纲，情况会更好。

## 2023年12月 第2套 · 给孩子自由（正面）

Nowadays parents are increasingly aware that allowing kids more freedom to explore and learn on their own helps foster their independence and boost their confidence. Could you imagine how utterly distinct a child's eighteenth birthday would be without a single decision of his own? Admittedly, giving children freedom is a complicated and lengthy process, but the achievements often bring tremendous social and economic benefits.

The pluses associated with this topic are numerous and substantial, yielding profound implications if widely adopted and sustainable over the long run. First, in terms of personal qualities, freedom teaches judgement better than instruction does. A good case in point is a child allowed to choose her own weekend project: she learns to estimate time, to abandon a bad idea and to finish what she started. Second, in contrast to a schedule arranged by adults, a small free space forces a child to decide and to live with the result. Third, without such practice, independence arrives late, often in the form of a painful first failure at university.

It is highly necessary for parents to let children manage one small part of their week alone. It would do better if mistakes were allowed while they are still cheap.

**译文**

如今父母越来越意识到，给孩子更多自由去自主探索和学习，有助于培养独立性、增强自信。如果从来没有一个属于自己的决定，一个孩子的十八岁生日会多么不同？诚然，给孩子自由是一个复杂而漫长的过程，但它带来的成就往往会产生巨大的社会和经济效益。

与本主题相关的好处众多且重大，若被广泛采用并长期持续，将产生深远影响。就个人品质而言，自由比说教更能教出判断力。一个典型例子是：被允许自己选周末项目的孩子，会学着估时间、放弃糟糕的点子、把开了头的事做完。与大人安排好的时间表相比，一小块自由的空间迫使孩子做决定并承担结果。没有这样的练习，独立会来得很晚，往往以大学里第一次痛苦的失败的形式到来。

父母极有必要让孩子独自管理一周里的一小块时间。如果能在代价还便宜的时候允许犯错，情况会更好。

## 2023年12月 第3套 · 老年人贡献（正面）

With their valuable skills and experience, elderly people can continue to make significant contributions to society. Could you imagine how utterly distinct a family's daily life would be without the help of grandparents? Admittedly, making full use of the elderly is a complicated and lengthy process, but the achievements often bring tremendous social and economic benefits.

The pluses associated with this topic are numerous and substantial, yielding profound implications if widely adopted and sustainable over the long run. First, in terms of personal abilities, experience solves problems that data cannot. A good case in point is a retired engineer who can tell from a sound whether a machine is about to fail, and a grandmother who recognises a fever a first-time parent cannot. Second, in contrast to a society that treats the old as obsolete, one that keeps them involved gains free childcare, steady volunteers and a living memory. Third, without them, the young spend years relearning what their parents already knew.

It is highly necessary for cities to open courses and volunteer posts to retired people. It would do better if their skills were passed on in schools and workshops rather than lost quietly at home.

**译文**

凭借宝贵的技能和经验，老年人可以继续为社会作出重要贡献。如果没有祖辈的帮忙，一个家庭的日常生活会多么不同？诚然，充分发挥老年人的作用是一个复杂而漫长的过程，但它带来的成就往往会产生巨大的社会和经济效益。

与本主题相关的好处众多且重大，若被广泛采用并长期持续，将产生深远影响。就个人能力而言，经验能解决数据解决不了的问题。一个典型例子是：退休工程师听声音就知道机器是不是快坏了，奶奶能看出发烧而新手父母看不出来。与把老人当成过时之物的社会相比，让他们继续参与的社会得到了免费看护、稳定的志愿者和活着的记忆。没有他们，年轻人要花好几年重新学父母早就懂的东西。

城市极有必要向退休人员开放课程和志愿岗位。如果他们的本领能在学校和工坊里传下去，而不是在家里悄悄失传，情况会更好。
